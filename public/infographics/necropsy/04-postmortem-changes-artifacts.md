### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Postmortem Changes & Artifacts — the Diagnostic Traps

**BIOLOGY OF DYING (blue, top timeline):** O₂ stops → ATP drops → ion pumps fail → cells swell → membranes break → enzymes digest tissue (autolysis) → bacteria proliferate (putrefaction). *Separate TRUE disease, TERMINAL change, POSTMORTEM artifact.*

## FOUR POSTMORTEM CHANGE CARDS
- **Rigor mortis (blue):** ATP depletion → actin-myosin locked. Onset 1–4 h, full 6–12 h, gone 24–36 h. Absent early → sepsis/cachexia
- **Livor mortis / hypostasis (red highlight):** gravity-settled blood on the **dependent side** → dark red-purple. **NOT hemorrhage**
- **Autolysis (blue):** self-digestion; fastest in pancreas/stomach/gut/brain. Softening + sloughing but **NO inflammation/fibrin/vascular reaction**
- **Putrefaction (blue):** bacterial overgrowth → green, gassy, foul; obscures lesions + ruins culture

## YELLOW COMPARISON STRIP — THE TRAPS
| Artifact | Looks like | The tell |
|---|---|---|
| Hypostasis | Hemorrhage | True hemorrhage INFILTRATES tissue; hypostasis only stains dependent surface |
| Autolysis | Enteritis | Enteritis has hyperemia + fibrin + reaction; autolysis has none |
| Postmortem aspiration | Pneumonia | True aspiration = inflammation/fibrin; postmortem = material, no reaction |
| Postmortem gas | Bloat/obstruction | Reactive, inflamed tissue = antemortem; none = postmortem |

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Airway content ALONE never diagnoses pneumonia — correlate with lung tissue reaction
- **Iatrogenic artifacts** (rib fractures, knife marks, organ tears): "If you caused it, document it"
- Record time of death AND necropsy — every interpretation depends on the interval

**GRAY FOOTER:** inflammation/fibrin/vascular reaction is the dividing line between postmortem change and true disease.
