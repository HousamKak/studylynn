### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Septicemia & Systemic Inflammatory Disease

**DEFINITION BOX (green, top):** Septicemia = organisms/toxins multiplying in the BLOODSTREAM → a whole-body vascular/inflammatory syndrome → diffuse endothelial injury, vascular leakage, coagulation abnormalities, hypoxia → lesions in MULTIPLE organs at once.

**THE CLASSIC SEPTIC PATTERN (red, central — memorize):** petechiae/ecchymoses + **enlarged spleen** + **dark unclotted blood** + congested organs + edema + **fibrinous inflammation** + swollen lymph nodes. *Think SYSTEMIC, not one organ.*

## MECHANISM CARDS (blue)
- **Endotoxemia:** LPS → cytokine storm → vascular collapse (horses especially prone)
- **DIC:** microthrombi + consumed clotting factors → simultaneous thrombosis AND hemorrhage; diffuse petechiae + infarcts + unclotted blood
- **Shock organs:** lung (edema) · liver (centrilobular necrosis) · kidney (tubular necrosis)
- **Septic emboli:** RANDOM multifocal abscesses (lung/liver/kidney/brain) = hematogenous, not airway

**ORGAN FINDINGS:** Spleen → enlarged, dark, congested, friable. Lymph nodes → enlarged, wet, hyperemic. Serosa → fibrinous peritonitis/pleuritis/pericarditis (polyserositis).

**YELLOW — SEPTICEMIA vs TOXEMIA & SPECIES:** Septicemia = organisms in blood; toxemia = toxins in blood, organisms localized. Horses → endotoxemia; cattle → fibrinous polyserositis; poultry → splenomegaly.

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Petechiae + big spleen + dark unclotted blood + fibrin = septicemia (pattern recognition)
- Sources: neonatal (umbilical / failure of passive transfer), GI, respiratory, uterine (metritis/pyometra), wounds
- Random multifocal abscesses ⇒ bloodstream spread

**GRAY FOOTER:** systemic not focal · spleen + petechiae + fibrin + dark blood · endotoxemia/DIC/shock organs · sample spleen/liver/lung/blood/node (fresh + fixed).
