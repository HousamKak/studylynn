### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** GI & CNS Dissection

## LEFT — GI TRACT (blue)
- **Stomach (monogastric):** cut along greater curvature; **observe contents BEFORE removing** (toxin/foreign-body evidence)
- **Ruminant forestomach (4):** rumen (content/pH/fermentation) · **reticulum → hardware disease** · omasum · **abomasum → ulcers/parasites**
- **Intestines:** open along the **ANTIMESENTERIC border**, segment by segment, with scissors
  - Thick wall → chronic inflammation; thin → necrosis; blood/parasites in content
- **Mesenteric nodes (check first):** enlarged+pale → lymphoma; enlarged+hyperemic → infection
- **GI integration — classify:** mechanical / inflammatory / toxic / parasitic / secondary-to-systemic

## RIGHT — CNS (blue)
- **Why special:** brain is soft, autolyzes fast, easily lacerated, often needs histology (minimal gross, major microscopic)
- **Biosafety:** rabies/zoonotic suspected → **NO power saws / aerosols**
- **Technique:** open vault carefully (don't penetrate → cortex laceration); elevate frontal lobes, cut cranial nerves, sever brainstem, **lift slowly — never pull**
- **Sectioning:** serial transverse → ventricles, white vs gray, hemorrhage, necrosis, masses

**YELLOW — CNS LESION PATTERNS:** Cloudy/fibrin meninges → meningitis · Flattened gyri + herniation → cerebral edema/↑ICP · Enlarged ventricles + thin cortex → hydrocephalus

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Opening intestine too early → contamination (sample sterile first)
- Antimesenteric border, segment by segment
- Rabies → no aerosols/power saws; never pull the brain forcefully (self-inflicted artifact)

**GRAY FOOTER:** GI — observe contents, antimesenteric cut, check nodes, classify. CNS — preserve, biosafety, gentle removal, serial sections.
