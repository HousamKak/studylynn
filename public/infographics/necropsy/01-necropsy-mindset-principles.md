### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** The Necropsy Mindset & 4 Governing Principles

**DEFINITION BOX (green, top):**
- **Necropsy** = a STRUCTURED diagnostic procedure after death to establish cause + mechanism of death, define morphologic diagnoses, determine etiology, and translate findings into clinical action
- "Aimless cutting and hacking has no place"

**CAUSE vs MECHANISM (blue, central comparison):**
- **Cause of death** = the disease
- **Mechanism of death** = the terminal physiology → shock, hypoxia, hemorrhage, arrhythmia
- Clinical medicine asks "what does the patient have?"; necropsy asks "what failed first, and what killed them last?"

**THE MINDSET (blue):**
- Before any incision: a **differential list** + a **sampling plan** + a **contamination plan**
- A systematic survey, NOT a search for one expected lesion

## THE 4 GOVERNING PRINCIPLES (4 numbered cards)
1. **Standard routine every time** → prevents diagnostic bias / tunnel vision; catches concurrent disease
2. **Minimal disturbance / contamination** → sample sterile sites BEFORE opening gut (torsions reduce, fluid shifts, fibrin detaches, gas escapes once you move organs)
3. **Reconstructability of organs** → cut with intention, not curiosity; preserve anatomic relationships + forensic credibility
4. **Best possible specimens** → bad samples (too thick / contaminated / delayed / mixed) create FALSE NEGATIVES

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Necropsy is **IRREVERSIBLE** — you cannot undo contamination, restore organ position, or re-collect a missed sample
- Name BOTH the cause AND the mechanism of death
- Tunnel vision (focusing on the expected organ) misses neoplasia, endocarditis, septic emboli

**GRAY FOOTER (summary):**
- Structured survey > targeted search · sterile-first · intentional cutting · best specimens
- Cause = disease; mechanism = shock/hypoxia/hemorrhage/arrhythmia
