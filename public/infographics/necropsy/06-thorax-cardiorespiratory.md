### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Thorax & Cardiorespiratory Dissection

**ENTRY BOX (green, top):** Open the **diaphragm first** and watch the lungs. Normal lungs collapse slightly (negative pressure lost). **Failure to collapse → adhesions / effusion / pneumothorax / consolidation.**

## IN-SITU THORACIC SURVEY (5 things in order)
1. **Pleural cavity** — fluid type (below)
2. **Pericardium** — fluid/fibrin/blood
3. **Lungs** in situ — distribution
4. **Mediastinum & vessels**
5. **Diaphragm** integrity

**YELLOW — CAVITY FLUID KEY:** Clear/straw → transudate (CHF) · Turbid → exudate (infection) · Fibrin → fibrinous serositis (bacterial) · Blood → hemothorax/hemopericardium (trauma, rupture, neoplasia, coagulopathy)

## HEART — BLOOD-FLOW METHOD (blue, central diagram)
- Assess size/shape first (dilation? right vs left predominance?)
- Open following flow: **RA → RV → pulmonary artery**; **LA → LV → aorta**
- Valves: thickening, **vegetations (endocarditis)**, ruptured chordae
- Myocardium: pallor (ischemia), hemorrhage, scars (fibrosis)
- Great vessels: thrombi, tears, aneurysm

## LUNG PATTERN KEY (high-yield)
- **Cranioventral consolidation** → bacterial bronchopneumonia
- **Diffuse wet + frothy** → pulmonary edema (heart failure)
- **Multifocal random nodules** → septic emboli / neoplasia
- **Diffuse 'meaty' non-collapsing** → severe pneumonia

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **Open the heart along BLOOD FLOW, never randomly** (random slicing destroys valve evidence)
- Remove heart + lungs together to preserve thrombus attachment + airway continuity
- Airway content alone ≠ pneumonia — correlate with lung tissue reaction

**GRAY FOOTER:** observe before disturb · diaphragm first · 5-point survey · blood-flow heart · distribution names the lung disease.
