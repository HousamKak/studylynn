### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Respiratory Pathology Patterns at Necropsy

**PRINCIPLE BOX (green, top):** Lung diagnosis depends on **DISTRIBUTION, not color**. A dark lung alone means little. Normal lung = pink, spongy, light, **collapses** when the thorax is opened.

## LUNG-DISTRIBUTION KEY (blue — lung silhouettes)
| Pattern | Disease |
|---|---|
| **Cranioventral consolidation** | Bacterial bronchopneumonia (airway spread) |
| **Cranioventral + foreign material** | Aspiration pneumonia (necrotizing) |
| **Diffuse, heavy, rubbery, non-collapsing** | Interstitial pneumonia (viral/toxic) |
| **Multifocal RANDOM nodules** | Embolic pneumonia (hematogenous) |
| **Heavy wet + frothy trachea** | Pulmonary edema (often left-CHF) |
| **Overinflated, pale, dry, crepitant** | Emphysema (interstitial form classic in cattle) |

## PLEURAL & AIRWAY (blue)
- **Pleuritis:** fibrin, adhesions, turbid fluid
- **Pneumothorax:** air in pleural cavity → lungs collapse (lost negative pressure)
- **Always open the trachea:** foam (edema), blood, exudate, parasites (lungworms), foreign material

**YELLOW — DISTRIBUTION LOGIC:** Cranioventral ⇒ airway spread · Diffuse ⇒ interstitial/wall disease · Random multifocal ⇒ hematogenous (find the source: endocarditis, hepatic/umbilical abscess).

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- DISTRIBUTION names the disease — color is unreliable
- Heavy wet lungs + tracheal froth = pulmonary edema (left-CHF)
- Sampling: histo = lesion edge + normal; culture = deep sterile; virology = fresh refrigerated

**GRAY FOOTER:** distribution > color · cranioventral/diffuse/multifocal · edema = wet+froth · always open trachea · sample edge+normal.
