### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Opening the Abdomen — Systematic Survey

**SETUP BOX (green, top):** Position — ruminant = **LEFT** lateral recumbency; monogastric = **RIGHT** lateral. Lift the wall away from viscera, incise the linea alba, reflect — then **STOP**.

**THE 30-SECOND RULE (blue, central):** Observe only — organ **position, size, color**, **fluid, gas, fibrin, hemorrhage, mesenteric tension**. This first view is anatomically authentic; once you manipulate, authenticity is gone.

## SYSTEMATIC ASSESSMENT (cards)
- **Fluid:** quantify + describe color/clarity/odor (sample sterilely before opening gut)
- **Organ position:** GDV → rotated stomach + displaced spleen; mesenteric volvulus → twisted root + dark bowel; diaphragmatic hernia → organs in thorax
- **Color/congestion:** diffuse dark → shock/hypoxia; pale liver → anemia; patchy → infarct/necrosis
- **Fibrin & adhesions:** *fibrin = inflammation until proven otherwise*; acute = fibrin + turbid fluid; chronic = dense adhesions
- **Gas:** uniform = postmortem; segmental + congestion = obstruction/torsion

## ORGAN-BY-ORGAN (mini-cards)
- **Liver** — 'pale liver' is nonspecific (fat/necrosis/lymphoma/toxin → histology)
- **Spleen** — enlarged + congested = septic pattern
- **Kidney** — capsule won't strip + lost corticomedullary detail → chronic disease; cut longitudinally
- **Bladder** — collect urine sterilely (toxicology)
- **Reproductive** — pyometra, torsion (often missed)
- **Lymph nodes** — enlarged+pale → lymphoma; enlarged+hyperemic → infection

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Sample sterile sites + effusion BEFORE opening intestine (contamination is permanent)
- **Ascites + hepatomegaly → predict right-sided CHF** → confirm in thorax ("make the abdomen talk to the thorax")

**GRAY FOOTER:** observe 30 s → fluid → position → color → fibrin → gas → organ-by-organ → correlate to thorax.
