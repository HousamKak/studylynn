## FIVE DISEASE CARDS

### CARD 1 — Vitamin E / Selenium Deficiency Degeneration

- **Affected tissues:** tongue muscle, skeletal muscle, **cardiac muscle**
- **Gross (red highlight):**
  - **White lesions**
  - **"Cooked meat" appearance**
- **Mechanism (blue):**
  - Vit E + Se normally protect membranes against oxidation
  - Without them → membrane damage → muscle fiber degeneration
- **Clinical:**
  - Weakness, locomotor disorders
  - Nutritional problems
  - **Sudden death** if cardiac muscle involved
- **Species:** ruminants, horses, pigs
- **Memory:** "Without antioxidants, muscles cook themselves from oxidative damage"

### CARD 2 — Circumscribed Calcinosis

- **Definition:** focal **dystrophic mineralization** caused by **local tissue injury**
- **Causes:** tongue biting, rough plants, pressure points, repeated trauma
- **Mechanism (blue):** damaged tissue becomes calciphilic → Ca salts precipitate locally
- **Characteristics:**
  - **Hard chalky white nodules**
  - Calcium phosphate / carbonate deposits
  - Persistent
  - Secondary inflammation by macrophages
- **Species:** more common in **dogs**
- **⚠️ KEY DISTINCTION (red box):**
  - **Dystrophic mineralization** = NORMAL blood calcium
  - **Metastatic mineralization** = HYPERCALCEMIA
- **Memory:** "Repeated trauma slowly turns tissue into chalk-like stone"

### CARD 3 — Petechial Macules

- Tiny **pinpoint hemorrhages** on gingiva
- **Associated with (red):**
  - **Septicemia**
  - **Toxemia**
  - **DIC** (disseminated intravascular coagulation)
- **Mechanism (blue):** endothelial injury → vascular leakage → hemorrhage
- **Clinical importance:** oral exam can reveal **systemic infection**
- **Memory:** "Sepsis sprays tiny red dots over the gums"

### CARD 4 — Uremic Ulcers

- Seen during **kidney failure** + severe **uremia**
- **Mechanism cascade (blue, 4-step):**
  1. Urea accumulates in **saliva**
  2. Oral bacteria convert urea → **ammonia**
  3. Ammonia **chemically burns** oral epithelium
  4. **Ulcers form**
- **Additional:** urea damages endothelium → vasculitis + thrombosis
- **Characteristics:**
  - Oral ulcers
  - Necrosis
  - **Severe halitosis** ("uremic breath")
  - Tongue + palate lesions
- **Memory:** "Kidney failure makes the mouth chemically burn itself"

### CARD 5 — Jaundice

- **Definition:** yellow discoloration from **bilirubin accumulation**
- **Three types (color-coded):**
  - **Pre-hepatic** → excessive hemolysis
  - **Hepatic** → liver dysfunction
  - **Post-hepatic** → bile obstruction
- **Oral manifestations:**
  - Yellow mucous membranes
  - **Saffron** coloration
  - **Orange** coloration if congestion present
- **Related (yellow comparison):**
  - **Cyanosis** = bluish mucosa from poor oxygenation
- **Memory:** "Bilirubin paints the mucosa yellow"
