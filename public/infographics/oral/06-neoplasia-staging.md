### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Oral Neoplasia & Tumor Staging

---

**OVERVIEW BOX (green, top):**
- Oral tumors span the spectrum from **benign (viral, slow-growing)** to **aggressive malignant** lesions with bone destruction and distant metastasis
- Always determine: **benign vs malignant**, then **stage** the disease
- Key categories: epithelial (papilloma, SCC), gingival (epulis), mesenchymal (fibrosarcoma)

---

## FIVE TUMOR CARDS (arranged on a benign → malignant gradient)

### CARD 1 — Papillomatosis (BENIGN, green frame)

- **Agent:** **Papillomavirus**
- **Characteristics:**
  - **Wart-like** benign masses
  - Common in **young dogs**
  - Often **multiple** lesions
- **Behavior:** benign, often self-resolving

### CARD 2 — Epulis (BENIGN, green frame)

- **Type:** **benign gingival tumor**
- **Characteristics:**
  - **Firm gingival mass**
  - Interferes with chewing
  - Usually slow-growing

### CARD 3 — Squamous Cell Carcinoma (MALIGNANT, red frame)

- **Type:** malignant **epithelial** tumor
- **Two gross forms:**
  - **Terebrating invasive form** (drilling, deeply invasive)
  - **Nodular exophytic form** (raised, outward-growing)
- **Characteristics:**
  - **Ulceration**
  - **Bone invasion**
  - Local tissue destruction
  - Aggressive behavior
- **Memory:** "Cancer drills through oral tissue and bone"

### CARD 4 — Fibrosarcoma (MALIGNANT, red frame)

- **Type:** malignant **connective tissue** tumor
- **Characteristics:**
  - **Aggressive local invasion**
  - **Rapid growth**
  - **Difficult surgical control** (poorly defined margins)

### CARD 5 — Extremely Malignant Tumors (DARK RED frame)

**Features:**
- Bone destruction
- Lymph node infiltration
- **Distant metastasis**
- Severe tissue invasion

**YELLOW COMPARISON STRIP — BENIGN vs MALIGNANT FEATURES**

| Feature | Benign | Malignant |
|---|---|---|
| Growth rate | Slow | Rapid |
| Margins | Well-defined | Poorly defined |
| Bone invasion | No | Yes (SCC, fibrosarcoma) |
| Ulceration | Rare | Common |
| Metastasis | No | Possible (stage 3–4) |
| Examples | Papilloma, epulis | SCC, fibrosarcoma |

---

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **SCC = #1 malignant oral tumor** — drills through bone
- **Fibrosarcoma** = recurs after surgery because margins are unclear
- **Stage 4 = distant metastasis** = poorest prognosis
- **Papillomatosis in young dogs** = benign, often self-resolves
- **Epulis** = gingival but benign — easily confused with malignant masses, biopsy needed

---

**GRAY FOOTER (summary pearls):**
- Benign tumors: papilloma, epulis
- Locally aggressive epithelial malignancy: SCC
- Locally aggressive mesenchymal malignancy: fibrosarcoma
- Staging tells prognosis better than tumor type alone
- Bone invasion + lymph node + lungs = Stage 4
