## FOUR DISEASE CARDS (one per anomaly)

### CARD 1 — Palatoschisis / Cheiloschisis

- **Palatoschisis:** congenital **failure of fusion** of the two halves of the palate during embryogenesis → abnormal communication between **oral and nasal cavities**
- **Cheiloschisis** ("harelip"): failure of **lip** fusion — may occur together with palatoschisis
- **Species commonly affected:**
  - **Charolais cattle**
  - **Dogs**
- **Clinical consequences:**
  - Poor suckling (no proper suction)
  - Milk enters nasal cavity and lungs
  - **Aspiration pneumonia**
  - Stunted growth + malnutrition
- **⚠️ Outcome (red):** often severe enough that affected animals are **euthanized**
- **Memory:** "Roof of the mouth never finished construction — milk leaks upward into the lungs"

### CARD 2 — Prognathism & Brachygnathism

- **Prognathism** = abnormally **long upper jaw**
- **Brachygnathism** = abnormally **short lower jaw**
- Result: **malocclusion** (teeth misalignment)
- **Consequences (mechanism cascade in blue):**
  - Teeth traumatize gums
  - Repeated erosion + ulceration
  - Pain during chewing
  - Secondary bacterial infection
  - Reduced food intake → weight loss
- **Species:** dogs, sheep, multiple livestock species
- **Memory:** "Jaws like badly aligned scissors damaging gums continuously"

### CARD 3 — Ectopic Hairs

- Hairs developing in **abnormal locations** (e.g. tongue)
- **Mechanism (blue):** oral mucosa + skin share embryologic origin → rare abnormal hair development
- **Course:**
  - Rare
  - **Incidental finding**
  - No major clinical consequence
- **Memory:** "Hair got lost and grew inside the mouth"

### CARD 4 — Epitheliogenesis Imperfecta (Epithelialization Defect)

- Congenital defect: portions of oral mucosa fail to develop properly
- **Characteristics:**
  - Missing mucosal covering
  - **Exposed underlying muscle**
  - Increased bacterial invasion risk
  - **Pink-brown depressed lesions**
- **Species:** piglets, cattle, horses
- **Inheritance:** **autosomal recessive**
- **Memory:** "Tongue born without its protective armor"
