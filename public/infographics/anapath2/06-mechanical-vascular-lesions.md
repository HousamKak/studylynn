### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Mechanical, Vascular & Congenital Intestinal Lesions

**CONGENITAL (green):** Atresia (missing segment/mucosa → occlusion; imperforate anus → megacolon) · Meckel's diverticulum (vitelline remnant; incidental, risky only if it twists) · **Megacolon** (congenital = Hirschsprung/absent myenteric plexus; acquired = stenosis/nerve damage).

**OBSTRUCTIONS (blue):** Enteroliths (struvite stone, horses/Arabians, pelvic flexure) · Impactions (meconium; worm-ball after deworming) · Cicatricial stenosis (pig salmonellosis → thrombosis → fibrosis → megacolon).

**THE SHARED VASCULAR SEQUENCE (red, central arrows):**
twist/telescoping/strangulation → **venous obstruction → passive congestion → edema → ischemic necrosis** → dark red→black wall → death
- **Intussusception** (dogs) — telescoping; irritation + hypermotility; do NOT pull → enterectomy
- **Torsion/volvulus** — mesenteric twist; often fatal
- **Hernia** — internal (horse epiploic foramen → strangulation) · external (diaphragmatic commonest → dyspnea)
- **Strangulation** — pedunculated mesenteric lipoma (horses)

**PARALYTIC ILEUS (blue):** non-mechanical hypomotility — shock, uremia, peritonitis, hypokalemia, toxemia, pain (mainly dogs).

**REPERFUSION / CRUSH SYNDROME (red, high-yield):** under stasis cells survive; on **reperfusion**, massive **Ca²⁺ influx** kills them → toxin release → rapid death. Treat volvulus with rehydration + chlorpromazine.

**YELLOW — TRUE vs POSTMORTEM INTUSSUSCEPTION:** true = hyperemia/fibrin, hard to reduce · postmortem = none, reduces easily.

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Venous occlusion → congestion → ischemic necrosis is the common pathway
- Reperfusion → Ca²⁺ influx → cell death (don't just restore flow)
- Rectal prolapse: reduce promptly (dries → necrosis)

**GRAY FOOTER:** congenital (atresia/megacolon) + obstruction + twist/hernia → congestion→necrosis; reperfusion kills on reflow.
