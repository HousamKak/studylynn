### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Gastric Ulcers, Gastritis, Parasites & Tumors

**LESION DEPTH LADDER (green):** Erosion (superficial) → Ulcer (to muscularis mucosae) → Perforating ulcer (whole wall → peritonitis). Old ulcer = thick fibrotic raised edges.

**THE BALANCE — ULCER PATHOGENESIS (blue, central scale):**
- **HCl aggression** vs **mucosal protection** (epithelium + mucus + **PGE2** + TGF-α/EGF + competent pylorus)
- Imbalance → acid burn → HCl reaches lamina propria → nerves + endocrine cells → **gastrin → more HCl** → vicious cycle
- **Contributing factors (red):** bile-salt reflux, alcohol, **stress** (pigs/racehorses), ischemia, **NSAIDs (↓PGE2)**, gastrin/histamine tumors (mast cell tumor in dogs)

**SPECIES PATTERNS (yellow):** Horses — competition stress/NSAIDs (40–90% have ulcer syndrome) · Dogs — NSAIDs/mast cell tumor · Ruminants — diet/BVD, rarely perforate.

**UREMIC GASTRITIS (blue):** uremia → vascular damage → hemorrhagic + erosive gastritis + **mucosal mineralization**.

**PARASITES & TUMORS (blue):**
- **Haemonchus** (barber-pole, abomasum) → anemia · **Ostertagia** (glandular nodules) · **Gasterophilus** (bot, non-glandular honeycomb ulcers)
- **Horse** = squamous cell carcinoma (non-glandular) → perforation · **Carnivores** = adenocarcinoma (glandular) + lymphoma (mucosal/submucosal thickening)

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- PGE2 is the key cytoprotector → NSAIDs cause ulcers
- Mucosal mineralization → think uremic gastritis
- Horse stomach tumor = squamous (non-glandular); carnivore = adeno/lymphoma

**GRAY FOOTER:** ulcer = HCl > protection (vicious cycle); uremia → mineralized erosions; abomasal worms (Haemonchus/Ostertagia); species-specific gastric tumors.
