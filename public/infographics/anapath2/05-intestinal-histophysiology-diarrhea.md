### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Intestinal Histophysiology & the 4 Diarrhea Mechanisms

**ABSORPTIVE ARCHITECTURE (green):** four nested scales — **microvilli → villi → circular folds → loops**. Absorption mainly duodenum + jejunum; large intestine has no villi. **Core principle: lose exchange surface → diarrhea.**

**CELL POPULATIONS (blue, villus/crypt diagram, ~3-day renewal from crypt enteroblasts):**
- **Mature enterocytes** (villus) — brush border + digestive enzymes → absorption
- **Immature crypt enterocytes** — SECRETE water/electrolytes/bicarbonate; toxins switch them on → hypersecretion
- **Goblet** (mucus) · **Paneth** (antibacterial) · **neuroendocrine** (gut hormones)
- **M cells** over Peyer's patches → entry portal for **Salmonella & BVD**

**THE 4 DIARRHEA MECHANISMS (yellow table):**
| Mechanism | Lesion |
|---|---|
| **Malabsorption** (MOST common) | brush-border/enterocyte loss in SI → ↓ surface |
| **Hypersecretion** | intact mucosa, hyperactive crypts (toxins) |
| **Exudation** | ↑ permeability → protein-losing enteropathy |
| **Hypermotility** | ↓ contact time (often parasites) |

**CONSEQUENCE CASCADE (red):** diarrhea → dehydration → hemoconcentration → hypovolemia → hypoperfusion → ketoacidosis → death (acidosis, or hypovolemic shock in the young).

**APPEARANCE CLUES (blue):** watery = small intestine · soft + mucus = colon · whitish pasty = malabsorption (milk) · yellow mucous = secretory · dark/bloody = invasive deep damage · greenish = parasitic.

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Malabsorption is the commonest mechanism
- M cells = Salmonella/BVD portal
- Watery = SI; soft+mucus = colon

**GRAY FOOTER:** surface loss drives diarrhea; 4 mechanisms (malabsorption/secretion/exudation/motility); read stool to localize.
