### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Infectious Enteritis II — Crypt, Chorion & Serosa

## CRYPT (ENTEROBLAST) DISEASES (blue)
- **Parvovirus / panleukopenia** — needs dividing cells; via Peyer's patches → **crypt enteroblast necrosis** → hyporegenerative villous atrophy + hemorrhagic diarrhea; **intranuclear inclusions**, lymphoid depletion, granular serosa; killed by superinfection; very environment-resistant
- **Lawsonia intracellularis** — crypt enteroblast **proliferation** (porcine proliferative enteropathy) → thickened mucosal folds; stain **Warthin-Starry**

## CHORION / SUBMUCOSA DISEASES (blue)
- **Johne's (paratuberculosis)** — M-avium-paratb in macrophages → chorion **macrophage sheets** → **encephaloid 'brain-like' folds**; stain **Ziehl-Neelsen**; cattle = diarrhea+wasting, small ruminants = wasting WITHOUT diarrhea
- **Salmonellosis** — 3 forms: **septicemic** (DIC) · **acute** (typhlocolitis) · **chronic** (ulcer+stenosis→megacolon)
- **Histiocytic ulcerative colitis** — Boxers <2 y; **PAS+ foamy macrophages**; colonic-type bloody mucoid feces
- **Equine rhodococcosis** — R. equi in macrophages (foals) → pyogranulomatous lymphadenitis + follicle-centered ulcers

## SEROSA DISEASE (blue)
- **FIP** — mutated coronavirus; Type III (Arthus) **vasculitis**; **wet** = yellow protein-rich effusion (humoral) · **dry** = serosal pyogranulomas (cellular); ~100% fatal

## IMMUNE / NON-INFECTIOUS (green)
- **Lymphangiectasia** — dilated lacteals → protein-losing enteropathy · **Lymphoplasmacytic (IBD) enteritis** — chorion lymphocyte/plasma-cell infiltrate → malabsorption (diet-responsive)

**YELLOW — STAIN/LAYER KEY:** Lawsonia = crypt / Warthin-Starry · Johne's = chorion macrophages / Ziehl-Neelsen · HUC = PAS+ macrophages.

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Parvo = crypt necrosis → hyporegenerative atrophy; Lawsonia = crypt proliferation
- Johne's encephaloid folds (Ziehl-Neelsen) vs Lawsonia thickened folds (Warthin-Starry)
- Salmonella 3 forms; FIP wet (humoral) vs dry (cellular), Type III vasculitis
