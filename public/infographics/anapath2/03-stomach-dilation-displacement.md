**TITLE (top band):** Gastric Dilation, GDV & Abomasal Displacement

## THREE SPECIES PANELS (blue)

- **🐴 Horse — gastric dilation & rupture:** impaired gastric emptying (obstruction/displacement) + **can't vomit** (strong gastroesophageal sphincter) → overfilling → dilation → **rupture**. Brown content (ingesta + clotted blood). Antemortem rupture = hemorrhage + fibrin at tear edges

- **🐶 Dog — gastric dilatation-volvulus (GDV):** large breeds; repeated dilation stretches the gastrohepatic ligament → mobile stomach → **torsion**. Triggers: bulky food, gas-promoting diet, exercise after meal, hereditary. Cascade: cardia+pylorus occluded → venous compression → hypoxia/acid-base imbalance → splenic ischemia → **arrhythmia → shock → death** (survival ~50%)
  - ⚠️ A 360° rotation may look 'normal' on imaging → diagnose on the marked dilation (red)

- **🐄 Cow — abomasal displacement/torsion:** atony + omental ligament rupture; **85% to the LEFT**; congestion → **very dark-red abomasum**; **'ping'** on percussion ('drop of water in a well'). Risk: high producers ≤6 wk post-calving, grain-fed calves; **hypocalcemia worsens atony**

**YELLOW — COMMON THREAD:** all involve impaired emptying/venous drainage → distension → ischemia.

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Horses can't vomit → dilation → rupture
- GDV: 360° twist can look normal on imaging → judge on dilation; survival ~50%
- Bovine abomasum displaces LEFT 85%; dark-red + 'ping'; hypocalcemia worsens atony

**GRAY FOOTER:** horse = dilation/rupture (no vomiting); dog = GDV (shock cascade); cow = left abomasal displacement (atony/hypocalcemia).
