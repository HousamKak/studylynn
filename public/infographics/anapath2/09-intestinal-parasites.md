### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Intestinal Parasites

## NEMATODES (blue)
- **Ascariasis** (Toxocara/Ascaris/Parascaris) — major **migration**: egg → liver (portal) → lungs → coughed up → swallowed → adults (white luminal roundworms); transmammary/transplacental. Mass die-off after deworming → **ileus** (horses); larvae ferry bacteria
- **Hookworm** (Ancylostoma/Uncinaria) — anticoagulant blood-feeder; re-attaches, old bites keep bleeding → **anemia + hypoproteinemia disproportionate to load**
- **Trichuriasis (whipworm)** — large intestine; thin anterior embedded + thick free posterior; usually mild
- **Oesophagostomosis** — larvae encyst in submucosa → dark **'lead-grain' granulomas**

## EQUINE STRONGYLES (blue)
- **Large — S. vulgaris (#1 verminous colic):** larvae migrate up arteries to the **cranial mesenteric artery** → arteritis → aneurysm → thromboembolism → intestinal **infarction**
- **S. equinus** (liver scars, non-fatal) · **S. edentatus** (→ **hemomelasma ilei**)
- **Small (cyathostomes)** — no migration; encyst in colon → mass emergence → profuse diarrhea

## CESTODES (blue)
- **Adults** in SI lumen → mostly deprivation, few lesions
- **Larval stages = serious:** coenurosis, cysticercosis, **echinococcosis → hydatid cysts** → liver failure/death

**YELLOW — ADULT vs LARVA:** adult tapeworms = mild (deprivation) · larvae = tissue cysts (dangerous).

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- S. vulgaris → cranial mesenteric arteritis/thromboembolism (verminous colic)
- Hookworm anemia far exceeds worm burden
- **NEVER rupture a hydatid cyst** — disseminates protoscolices
- S. edentatus → hemomelasma ilei

**GRAY FOOTER:** nematodes (ascarid migration, hookworm anemia, whipworm, oesophagostome nodules); equine strongyles (S. vulgaris colic); cestode larvae (hydatid).
