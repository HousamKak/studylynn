### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Hardware Disease, Fungal Ruminitis & Rumen Fluke

**FOREIGN BODIES (green):** Phytobezoars (plant) & trichobezoars (hair) = usually NO consequence. **Metallic** = dangerous (trapped in the reticulum).

**HARDWARE-DISEASE CASCADE (red, central arrow chain):**
Metal in reticulum → reticular contractions drive it deeper → **perforation** → **traumatic reticuloperitonitis** → migrates cranially → **traumatic pericarditis** → pericardial effusion → **cardiac tamponade** → heart can't fill → **cardiac arrest → death**
- Prophylaxis = swallowed **magnet** (not 100% effective)

**FUNGAL (MYCOTIC) RUMINITIS (blue):**
- Aspergillus / Mucor / Rhizopus invade blood vessels → destruction, hemorrhage, **thrombosis → ischemic necrosis** (severe, extensive)
- **Cockade / target lesion** (central diagram): center **necrosis** → ring of **hemorrhage** → peripheral **congestion**; same lesion in rumen + abomasum; star-shaped scars if survives

**RUMEN FLUKE (blue):** **Paramphistomum** — trematode whose adults resemble rumen papillae; snail-borne metacercariae on aquatic plants; adults usually low clinical significance

**YELLOW — BEZOAR vs METAL:** bezoars (plant/hair) = harmless · metal = perforation → reticuloperitonitis/pericarditis

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Metal → reticulum perforation → pericarditis → tamponade → death
- Cockade lesion (necrosis–hemorrhage–congestion) = angioinvasive fungi
- Mycotic ruminitis mortality 25–90% within 24 h

**GRAY FOOTER:** metal = hardware disease (→ tamponade); fungi = cockade/thrombotic necrosis; Paramphistomum = papilla-mimicking rumen fluke.
