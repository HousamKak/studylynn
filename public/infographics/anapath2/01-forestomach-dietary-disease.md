### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Forestomach — Anatomy & Dietary Disease

**THE THREE FORESTOMACHS (green, top):**
- **Rumen** — aglandular fermentation vat; squamous keratinized papillae (long on forage, short on concentrate) → VFA fermentation + absorption
- **Reticulum** — honeycomb pouch; mechanical, TRAPS heavy/metallic particles
- **Omasum** — leafed 'sandpaper' grinder (short abrasive papillae)

## DIETARY DISEASE CARDS
- **Tympanism (bloat) — blue:** Primary = legume FOAM blocks eructation (most common); Secondary = obstruction/vagal atony (can't eliminate gas). Distended rumen → compresses diaphragm/lungs + ↓ venous return → death
  - ⚠️ **Necropsy proof = esophageal CONGESTION LINE** (cranial red / caudal pale) → bloat was present in life (red)
- **Lactic acidosis & ruminitis — blue:** grain overload → ↑ Strep bovis/Lactobacillus → lactic acid → pH **< 5** → mucosal damage → A. pyogenes / F. necrophorum → ruminitis → portal bacteremia → **liver abscesses**. Osmotic water shift → dehydration + blood acidosis → collapse
- **Hyperparakeratosis — blue:** <10% forage → short, dark, clumped parakeratotic papillae (a feed-management flag)

**YELLOW — TWO DIRECTIONS OF DIET IMBALANCE:** gas → tympanism · pH drop + mucosal damage → acidosis/ruminitis

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Esophageal congestion line = antemortem bloat (pathognomonic at necropsy)
- Rumen pH < 5 = acidosis (normal 5.5–7.5)
- Ruminitis → examine the LIVER (portal abscesses)

**GRAY FOOTER:** rumen/reticulum/omasum roles; diet imbalance → bloat OR acidosis→ruminitis→liver abscess; low forage → hyperparakeratosis.
