## FIVE DRUG-CLASS CARDS

### CARD 1 — Lubricant Laxatives (oily substances)

- Drugs: **Mineral oil, Vaseline, Glycerin, Liquid paraffin**
- **Mechanism (blue):** coat feces with **water-immiscible film** → ↑ water content → easy passage
- **Species:** horses, cats, cattle
- **⚠️ Adverse (red):**
  - ↓ Absorption of **fat-soluble vitamins** (A, D, E, K)
  - **Foreign body reaction** if aspirated → **granulomatous colitis / pneumonia**
  - **Oil leak** past anal sphincter (cats — embarrassing for owner)

### CARD 2 — Hydrophilic Colloids / Bulk Laxatives (Dietary fibers)

- Drugs: **Methylcellulose, Psyllium, wheat bran, pumpkin, fruits**
- **Mechanism cascade (blue):**
  1. Nonabsorbed polysaccharides / cellulose → resistant to enzymatic digestion
  2. **Hydrophilic matrix** → attracts water
  3. ↑ Fecal bulk + ↑ luminal distension
  4. **Reflex peristalsis**
- Plus: cellulose / hemicellulose fermented in hind gut → **SCFA** (short-chain fatty acids) → prokinetic effect
- **Onset:** slow (> 24 h)
- **Side effect:** **bloating** (especially early)

### CARD 3 — Hyperosmotic Cathartics (Saline)

**Two subtypes:**
- **Saline laxatives:** Na2SO4, Mg salts (**Magnesium Sulfate = Epsom salt**)
- **Nonabsorbed sugars:** **Lactulose, Mannitol, Sorbitol**

- **Mechanism (blue):**
  - Solutes not absorbed → **draw fluid into lumen by osmosis**
  - ↑ Motility + peristalsis
  - Mucosal secretion → distension
- **Fast onset:** 1–3 h
- **Used for:**
  - Bowel cleansing
  - Endoscopy prep
  - Treatment of poisoning (gastric lavage prep)
- **Dissolved in water before use**
- **⚠️ Adverse (red):**
  - Overdose → excess fluid loss → dehydration
  - **Renal impairment** → Mg²⁺ accumulates → **hypermagnesemia → neuromuscular depression**

**🌟 SPECIAL CALLOUT — Lactulose (red/blue prominent box):**
- Fermented by colon bacteria → **lactic + acetic acid** → ↑H⁺
- Acidifies colon → NH3 → **NH4⁺** (charged, can't cross back) → **excreted**
- **↓ Liver detox needs**
- **Used for:**
  - **Hepatic encephalopathy** ⭐
  - Chronic constipation
- **Golytely** = combination osmotic prep product

### CARD 4 — Stimulant Laxatives (Irritants)

- Drugs:
  - **Bisacodyl** (diphenylmethane derivative — oral / suppository)
  - **Anthraquinones** (plants: aloe, cascara, **senna**)
  - **Ricinoleic acid** (castor oil)
- **Mechanism (blue):**
  - ↓ Na⁺/K⁺ ATPase in intestinal cells → ↑ electrolyte + water loss into lumen
  - Induces **low-grade inflammation** → ↑ motility
- **Onset:** 6–8 h (oral)
- **⚠️ Use at lowest dose + shortest duration (abuse risk)**
- **Anthraquinones** → urine color change (orange/red)
- Used for **bowel cleansing**

### CARD 5 — Stool Softeners (Surfactants)

- Drugs: **Docusates (sodium / calcium salts)**
- **Mechanism (blue):** surfactant → water accumulates in stool → softens
- **Safe** — but cramping at high dose
- **Onset:** slow (24–48 h)

---

## ⚠️ TOXICITY BOX (red, prominent — bottom-left)

**Long-term / abuse consequences:**
- **Dependence** + tolerance
- Severe diarrhea, abdominal **colic**
- **Water + electrolyte loss** → dehydration → shock
- **Secondary aldosteronism** (from chronic volume loss)
- ↑ Sensitivity in mucosa
- **Megacolon, flatulence**
- ↑ IBD risk
- **Protein-losing enteropathy**, **loss of Ca²⁺**, **steatorrhea**
- **Bone pain** from hypocalcemia

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **Lactulose** = first-line for **hepatic encephalopathy** (traps ammonia as NH4⁺)
- **Mg cathartics** → dangerous in **renal failure**
- **Mineral oil** → granulomatous pneumonia if aspirated → never force in recumbent animal
- **Bulk laxatives** → never give without adequate water (impaction risk)
- **Stimulant abuse** → megacolon, hypocalcemia, dependence
