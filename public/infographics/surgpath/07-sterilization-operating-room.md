### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Sterilization & the Operating Room

**DEFINITION BOX (green):** Sterilization destroys ALL microbial life INCLUDING bacterial spores (disinfection does not). "Only clean instruments can be effectively sterilized."

## 4-STEP PIPELINE (blue, left→right)
1. **Pre-disinfection** (right after use — stops organic matter drying on)
2. **Washing** (MOST important — detergent + mechanical action)
3. **Packaging** (after complete drying; let sterilant in, keep microbes out)
4. **Sterilization**

## STERILIZATION METHODS (table)
| Method | Cycle / note |
|---|---|
| Dry heat (Poupinel oven) | **180°C for 1 hour** |
| Moist heat (autoclave) | **134°C, 18 min, 2.1 bar** |
| Irradiation (gamma) | industrial disposables |
| Ethylene oxide | gas, low-temp, heat-sensitive items |
| Peracetic acid | oxidizing agent |
| Glutaraldehyde | now inappropriate/prohibited (toxicity) |

**VALIDATION (blue):** physical indicators (temp/pressure/time) · chemical indicators (visible COLOR change, widely used) · biological indicators (resistant microbes confirm kill).

**YELLOW — OPERATING ROOM:** forward-movement principle (clean → less clean) · separate clean/dirty circuits · 3-step cleaning (clean→wash→disinfect) · WET cleaning preferred (avoid vacuuming).

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Sterilization kills SPORES; disinfection does not
- Autoclave = 134°C / 18 min / 2.1 bar; dry heat = 180°C / 1 h
- Washing is the most important pre-sterilization step
- Glutaraldehyde is now largely prohibited

**GRAY FOOTER:** pre-disinfect → wash → package → sterilize → validate (chemical color change); OR uses forward flow + wet cleaning.
