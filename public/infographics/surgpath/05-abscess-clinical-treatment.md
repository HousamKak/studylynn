### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Abscess — Clinical, Evolution & Treatment

## CLINICAL FORMS (blue)
- **Superficial hot:** visible; circular central zone + inflammatory ring + outer edema (24–48 h); red/hot/painful MAXIMAL centrally. Suppuration → central softening + fluctuation (max-fluctuation point = pus closest to surface)
- **Deep subaponeurotic:** diffuse swelling/heat, VERY severe pain (rigid aponeurosis → high pressure)
- **Visceral:** no visible swelling; signs depend on the organ; diagnosed by imaging (US/CT; MRI for CNS) + neutrophilia

**EVOLUTION (blue):** Drainage → rapid improvement, healing by secondary intention (8–10 days). Deep pus tracks to least resistance → fistula (e.g. apical dental → suborbital fistula). No elimination → "cools down" → cold abscess. Retained foreign body → chronic suppuration.

**YELLOW — DIFFERENTIALS (swelling):** Hematoma (uniform, afebrile, aspirate = blood) · Hernia (reducible through the ring) · Cyst (non-inflamed, aspirate = clear fluid) · Tumor (variable signs → biopsy).

## TREATMENT BY PHASE (green)
- **Inflammatory phase:** COLD compresses + NSAIDs/analgesics + antibiotics (± anti-tetanus in carnivores)
- **Suppuration phase:** WARM compresses to ripen → puncture at complete fluctuation (max-fluctuation, dependent position); drainage is the PRIORITY (antibiotics penetrate pus poorly)
- **Visceral/cold:** surgical excision, marsupialization, or omentalization; hard cold → excision; soft cold → dependent puncture + drainage

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- COLD compresses (inflammatory) vs WARM compresses (suppuration) — opposite by phase
- Drainage > antibiotics once pus has formed
- Bloodstream spread is worst BEFORE the pyogenic membrane forms → septicemia/pyohemia
- Dangerous sites: pharyngeal (airway), hepatic, renal, cerebral

**GRAY FOOTER:** localize → ripen → drain at max fluctuation; differentials = hematoma/hernia/cyst/tumor; deep needs surgery.
