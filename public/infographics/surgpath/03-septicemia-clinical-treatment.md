### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Septicemia — Clinical, Diagnosis & Treatment

## CLINICAL SIGNS (blue)
- Fever >40°C + chills/sweating; drowsiness/depression; conjunctiva dark-red or yellowish
- Rapid shallow breathing; fast + WEAK pulse
- Worsening: adynamia, cold extremities, livid mucous membranes, profuse diarrhea

**TEMPERATURE CURVE (red highlight):** Day 1 sudden spike 38.5°C → 42°C. Days 2–3 temperature FALLS while the **pulse keeps rising** — this mismatch = circulatory collapse, a BAD prognostic sign (NOT recovery). Death may occur within 48 h from hyperthermia.

## DIAGNOSIS (blue)
- Severe rapid hyperthermia + SIRS (≥2 signs) ± DIC ± hypovolemia
- Find the entry point (skin, urinary, genital, respiratory)
- **Blood cultures (+++):** before antibiotics, during fever spikes; aerobic + anaerobic; isolate organism in only ~20%
- CBC: neutrophilia (abscess), leukopenia (severe), thrombocytopenia (DIC)

**YELLOW — DDx:** Gangrenous septicemia (spreading edema + gas crepitation). Pyohemia (slower, metastatic abscesses).

## TREATMENT (green)
- **Source control:** drain abscesses, surgery (peritonitis), remove infected catheters (>2 days)
- **Early broad-spectrum antibiotics:** β-lactam + aminoside ± glycopeptide → adapt to antibiogram
- **Septic shock:** crystalloids 33 mL/kg/1h then 20 mL/kg/h → albumin → transfusion (Hb<8); refractory → vasopressors (dopamine/dobutamine/adrenaline); O₂, insulin

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Falling temp + rising pulse = circulatory collapse (bad sign)
- Blood cultures BEFORE antibiotics; positive in only ~20%
- Antibiotics may transiently WORSEN signs (LPS release on bacterial kill)
- Prognosis is VERY reserved

**GRAY FOOTER:** hyperthermia + SIRS → cultures before antibiotics → source control + early broad-spectrum → shock resuscitation.
