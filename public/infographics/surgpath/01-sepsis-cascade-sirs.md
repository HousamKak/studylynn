### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** The Sepsis Cascade & SIRS

**DEFINITION BOX (green, top):** Septicemia = a severe GENERALIZED infectious disease — massive release of microorganisms AND their toxins into the blood, with rapid dissemination. (Contrast: a toxico-infection like tetanus stays localized and is toxin-driven.)

## THE ESCALATION LADDER (central, top → bottom, worsening perfusion)
- **SIRS** — systemic inflammatory response
- **Sepsis** = Infection + SIRS
- **Severe sepsis (septicemia)** = + hypotension (BP <90 or drop >40 mmHg despite fluids)
- **Septic shock** = persistent hypotension + hypoperfusion
- **Death**

**SIRS CRITERIA BOX (blue) — need ≥ 2:**
- Temperature >39.5°C or <37°C
- Heart rate >90/min (tachycardia)
- Respiratory rate >20/min (tachypnea/polypnea) or hypocapnia
- Leukocytes >12,000 or <4,000/mm³
- (± clotting abnormality)

**YELLOW — RELATED TERMS:** Bacteremia = bacteria in blood (NOT all → sepsis). Sepsis = the overlap of Infection + SIRS. SIRS can also come from trauma, burns, or acute hemorrhagic pancreatitis.

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Sepsis = Infection + SIRS; SIRS needs ≥ 2 criteria
- Severe sepsis = sepsis + hypotension despite fluids
- Septic shock = PERSISTENT hypotension + hypoperfusion
- Not every bacteremia is sepsis

**GRAY FOOTER:** SIRS → sepsis → severe sepsis → septic shock → death; each step worsens blood pressure/perfusion.
