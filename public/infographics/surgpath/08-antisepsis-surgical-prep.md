### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Antisepsis & Surgical Preparation

**SKIN ANTISEPSIS SEQUENCE (green):** clip site + pre-wash (prep room) → detergent wash (3–7×) → rinse → apply antiseptic ONLY on completely DRY skin → let it dry before incision.

## ANTISEPTIC FAMILIES (table)
| Family | Spectrum / key trait |
|---|---|
| Iodine derivatives | Broad incl. SPORICIDAL; inhibited by organic matter; short residual; avoid pregnancy/neonates |
| Chlorhexidine (biguanide) | Long action 12–24 h; works in organic matter; only SLIGHT sporicidal; avoid inner ear/meninges |
| Alcohol | Fast, NON-sporicidal, volatile; never on mucosa/open wounds; boosts other antiseptics |
| Chlorinated | Broad/sporicidal oxidizers; inhibited by organic matter; short shelf life |

**Selection:** chlorhexidine + povidone-iodine recommended; hydroalcoholic rubbing ≈ chlorhexidine scrub (faster/easier).

**ANTIBIOTIC PROPHYLAXIS (blue, timing axis):** broad-spectrum IV **~30 min BEFORE incision** → re-dose every two half-lives → stop by end of surgery (max 24 h). **Burke (1961):** given 3 h after contamination = NO benefit → must precede incision. Prophylaxis alone cannot prevent septic complications.

**YELLOW — SURGEON PREP:** skullcap + mask + scrubs + OR shoes · hand scrub hands→elbows, keep hands ABOVE elbows when rinsing · sterile gown (front + forearms sterile; back/shoulders/underarms NOT) · gloves after gown · draping = waterproof sterile field · Halsted's atraumatic principles.

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Apply antiseptic only to DRY skin
- Alcohol = NON-sporicidal, never on mucosa/wounds; chlorhexidine = long action, weak sporicidal
- Prophylactic antibiotic ~30 min BEFORE incision (Burke: useless 3 h after contamination)
- Gown sterile zones = front + forearms only

**GRAY FOOTER:** dry skin antisepsis → right antiseptic → antibiotic before incision → scrub/gown/glove/drape with discipline.
