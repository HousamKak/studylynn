### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Surgical Asepsis & Wound Classification

**DEFINITIONS (green, 2×2):**
- **Contamination** = harmful microbes present (people/equipment/environment)
- **Asepsis** = PREVENT microbes entering the surgical wound
- **Antisepsis** = reduce microbes on LIVING tissue
- **Disinfection** = same goal, on INERT surfaces

**SSI RISK EQUATION (blue, central):** SSI Risk = (Importance of Contamination × Virulence) ÷ Host Resistance. *Directly proportional to contamination & virulence; inversely proportional to host resistance.* Surgical infection depends on 3 elements: microbial source + patient + procedure.

## ALTEMEIER WOUND CLASSES (ladder with risk %)
| Class | Type | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| I | Clean (no septic cavity, no inflammation) | < 5% |
| II | Clean-contaminated (controlled septic-cavity entry) | 5–15% |
| III | Contaminated (fresh trauma / major aseptic breach) | 15–30% |
| IV | Dirty/Infected (pus, devitalized tissue, fecal contamination) | > 30% |

**YELLOW — RISK FACTORS:** Patient (immunosuppression, age extremes, diabetes, obesity, distant infection) · Contamination (quantity) · Virulence · Procedure (duration/complexity/asepsis).

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- SSI Risk = (Contamination × Virulence) ÷ Host Resistance
- Altemeier: I <5%, II 5–15%, III 15–30%, IV >30%
- Asepsis = prevention; antisepsis = living tissue; disinfection = inert surfaces
- Prevention spans 4 stages: premises/equipment → pre-op → intra-op → post-op

**GRAY FOOTER:** lower contamination/virulence or raise host resistance; asepsis is only as strong as its weakest link.
