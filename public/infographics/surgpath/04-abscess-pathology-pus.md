### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Abscess — Pathology, Pus & Zones

**DEFINITION BOX (green):** An abscess = a LOCALIZED infectious inflammatory process forming a purulent collection within tissue, with the classic signs of acute inflammation. Classes: superficial hot (SC, e.g. bovine whitlow), deep hot (subaponeurotic/visceral), and cold (soft/hard).

## TWO PHASES (blue)
- **Acute inflammatory phase:** trauma + germs → cellular destruction, congestion, plasma exudation (edema), diapedesis of PMNs + macrophages
- **Suppuration phase:** germs + necrotic tissue → leukocyte influx → pus in a cavity → pyogenic membrane forms

**THE 3 ZONES (central cross-section):**
- **Zone 1 — inner (liquid core):** pus, PMNs, germs, fibrin
- **Zone 2 — middle:** pyogenic membrane = granulation tissue (new vessels, embryonic cells)
- **Zone 3 — outer:** fibrous capsule (collagen, fibroblasts)

**PUS (blue):** ~8/10 liquid phase (plasma, enzymes, proteins — greasy) + ~2/10 cellular debris (dead PMNs, monocytes) ± active germs. Neutrophil lifespan 3–4 days → granulo-fatty degeneration → enzymes liquefy necrotic tissue → cavity.

**YELLOW — PUS APPEARANCE:** Gram+ = thick yellow greasy · hemolytic = red · Pseudomonas = bluish/lumpy. Bovine = pasty · rabbit = cheesy · bird = solid/chalky.

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- 3 zones: pus core → pyogenic membrane (granulation) → fibrous capsule
- Pyogenic membrane = HOST defense reaction (limits/walls off infection)
- Bluish lumpy pus → Pseudomonas; rabbit/bird = caseous (won't simply drain)
- Cold abscess = old abscess, acute inflammation gone (usually mistreated hot abscess)

**GRAY FOOTER:** inflammatory → suppuration phase; mature abscess = cavity/pus + pyogenic membrane + fibrous capsule.
