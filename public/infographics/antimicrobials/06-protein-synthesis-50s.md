**SHARED-SITE BOX (blue, top — the unifying concept):**
- **Macrolides, Lincosamides, Chloramphenicol** all bind the **50S** subunit at the **same / overlapping region**
- → **Cross-resistance** (ribosomal methylation) AND **antagonism** if combined
- Mostly **bacteriostatic** (cidal at high concentration)

---

## CARD 1 — MACROLIDES (green header)
- Drugs: **Erythromycin** (+ newer below)
- **Mechanism (blue):** reversibly bind **50S**, near the **chloramphenicol** site → bacteriostatic; **Gram +ve**
- Erythromycin does **NOT** penetrate the **BBB**
- ⚠️ **Erythromycin = CYP450 INHIBITOR** (red)
- **Adverse:** GI signs & vomiting (motilin/prokinetic effect); **diarrhea in horses**
- *Phenobarbital induces erythromycin metabolism*

**Newer macrolides:**
- **Clarithromycin** → **Rhodococcus equi pneumonia in foals** (PO with food, ± rifampin)
- **Tilmicosin** → long-acting, immunomodulatory, treats Mycoplasma — ⚠️ **CARDIOTOXIC** (fatal, esp. pigs/humans)

## CARD 2 — LINCOSAMIDES
- Drugs: **Clindamycin, Lincomycin**
- **Bacteriostatic**; **aerobes NOT affected** → great vs **anaerobes** + Gram +ve
- Bind **50S** (same region as macrolides/chloramphenicol)
- **Clindamycin** is **NOT** a substrate for macrolide **efflux pumps** → macrolide-resistant strains may stay clindamycin-susceptible
- Penetrates **bone** well (→ osteomyelitis); low CNS
- Liver metabolism (cats), fecal/GI excretion (dogs)

## CARD 3 — CHLORAMPHENICOL
- **Mechanism (blue):** binds **50S**, blocks **peptidyl-transferase** → no peptide bond; bacteriostatic
- **Highly lipophilic** → large Vd (**CNS, eye**); also hits **mammalian mitochondrial ribosomes** (→ toxicity)
- ⚠️ **CYP450 INHIBITOR**; **antagonistic with macrolides** (compete for same 50S site)
- **Resistance:** ↓ permeability, efflux, altered 50S binding, **nitroreductases**, **chloramphenicol acetyltransferase**

**Chloramphenicol Toxicity (red box):**
- (a) **Dose-related, reversible** ↓ erythroid protein synthesis → anemia
- (b) **Idiosyncratic, irreversible APLASTIC ANEMIA → fatal**
- **Cats most sensitive** (↓ glucuronidation)
- **Florfenicol** = food-animal analog → **no nitro group → no aplastic anemia**, resists acetylation

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **Erythromycin & chloramphenicol = CYP450 INHIBITORS** (drug interactions); rifampin is the inducer
- **Chloramphenicol → fatal idiosyncratic aplastic anemia**; **cats most sensitive** → **florfenicol** for food animals
- **Clarithromycin → R. equi foal pneumonia**; **tilmicosin = cardiotoxic**
- All three share the **50S site** → **cross-resistance + antagonism** (don't combine macrolide + chloramphenicol)
- **Clindamycin** escapes macrolide efflux pumps; great bone penetration

- Clindamycin = anaerobes + bone; clarithromycin = R. equi foals
