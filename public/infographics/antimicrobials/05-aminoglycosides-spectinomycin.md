### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Aminoglycosides & Spectinomycin

---

**CLASSIFICATION BOX (green, top):**
- **Class:** **30S** protein-synthesis inhibitor → **bactericidal**
- **Drug of choice for aerobic Gram −ve**
- Members (extended spectrum, anti-**Pseudomonas**): **Gentamicin, Amikacin, Tobramycin**
- **Polycations** → no oral absorption, limited distribution, excreted **rapidly unchanged renally**

---

## CARD 1 — MECHANISM (blue, the uptake cascade)
1. Drug diffuses through **porin channels** of the Gram −ve outer membrane
2. **O₂-DEPENDENT transport** carries it inside the cell
3. **Irreversibly binds 30S** proteins → corrupts **mRNA translation**
- Limited **Gram +ve** action → **don't use as single agents** against Gram +ve

## CARD 2 — PAE & ONCE-DAILY DOSING (blue + graph)
- Killing is **CONCENTRATION-dependent**
- Marked **Post-Antibiotic Effect (PAE)** → killing persists **after serum falls below MIC**
- → **One big dose, less frequently** works as well or better
- Also **reduces nephro/oto-toxicity** risk
- *(Contrast: β-lactams are time-dependent — opposite dosing logic)*

## CARD 3 — RESISTANCE — FOUR ROUTES (numbered)
1. **Failure to penetrate**
2. **Anaerobes intrinsically resistant** — uptake is **O₂-dependent** (also blocked by divalent cations, hyperosmolarity, ↓ pH, anaerobic conditions)
3. **Low ribosomal affinity**
4. **Modifying enzymes (MOST common)**
- ✅ **Amikacin** is modified by only a **few enzymes** → active against **gentamicin-resistant** strains

---

## TOXICITY (red box — class-wide):
- ⚠️ **Nephrotoxicity + Ototoxicity** (shared by the whole class)
- Once-daily dosing mitigates both

---

## SPECTINOMYCIN SIDEBAR (distinct panel)
- **Aminocyclitol** — non-glycosidic nucleus **like the AGs** → similar **PK**
- BUT **bacteriostatic** with a different action → **kidney-safe** (avoids AG nephro/oto-toxicity)
- Use: **food-producing animals (pigs)**, treats **E. coli**

| Dosing | Conc-dependent + PAE | — |

---

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **Nephro + oto-toxicity** = the class signature
- **Anaerobes are intrinsically resistant** — uptake needs **oxygen**
- **Modifying enzymes** = #1 resistance; **amikacin escapes most** → beats gentamicin-resistant bugs
- **Concentration-dependent + PAE** → **once-daily** big dose (opposite of β-lactams)
- Don't use AGs as **single agents** against Gram +ve

---

**GRAY FOOTER (summary pearls):**
- AG = aerobic Gram −ve DOC; O₂-dependent uptake → irreversible 30S binding
- Conc-dependent + long PAE → one big dose, less often
- Amikacin = the resistance-beater (few modifying enzymes)
- Spectinomycin = AG-like PK, bacteriostatic, kidney-safe (pigs / E. coli)
