### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Non-β-Lactam Cell-Wall Agents — Vancomycin & Bacitracin

---

**CLASSIFICATION BOX (green, top):**
- Both inhibit **cell-wall synthesis** but are **NOT β-lactams** (β-lactamase doesn't touch them)
- **Vancomycin** = glycopeptide → resistant **Gram +ve**
- **Bacitracin / Colistin** = polypeptides → mostly **topical** Gram +ve / systemic Gram −ve (colistin)
- ❌ **NOT used in food-producing animals**

---

## CARD 1 — VANCOMYCIN (glycopeptide, large panel)

**Mechanism (blue):**
- **Time-dependent** cell-wall synthesis inhibitor
- **Cidal** on Gram +ve **aerobes** (staphylococci, **MRSA**) + anaerobe **Clostridium**
- **Bacteriostatic** on **enterococcus**

**Spectrum (red):**
- ⚠️ **ALL Gram −ve are RESISTANT**

**Pharmacokinetics:**
- **IV ONLY**, by **slow infusion**

**Adverse effects (red ribbon):**
- **Histamine-related reactions** ("red-man"): **pruritus, tachycardia**, ± hypotension
- **Nephrotoxicity + ototoxicity** (newer formulations safer)

---

## CARD 2 — BACITRACIN & COLISTIN (polypeptide, large panel)

**Mechanism (blue):**
- **Bacitracin** = natural/semi-synthetic polypeptide → cell-wall inhibitor
- Covers **all Gram +ve**, **rare resistance**

**Pharmacokinetics:**
- **Very poor oral absorption**
- **Muscle + kidney affinity**; excreted **unchanged renally** → **nephrotoxic** systemically
- **↓ metabolism**

**Adverse effects (red ribbon):**
- **Nephrotoxic** (systemic use)
- **Neuromuscular blockade** → **respiratory paralysis**
- Pain at injection site

**Clinical use (green):**
- **Only colistin** is used **systemically**
- Bacitracin → **well tolerated locally** → mastitis, skin, eye, ear infections, enterocolitis

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **Vancomycin → ALL Gram −ve resistant**; IV-only, slow infusion (avoid red-man reaction)
- Vancomycin = **MRSA & C. difficile** reserve drug
- **Bacitracin = nephrotoxic systemically** → keep it **topical**
- Polypeptides → **neuromuscular blockade → respiratory paralysis**
- ❌ Neither class in **food animals**

- ALL Gram −ve resist vancomycin
- Bacitracin = topical Gram +ve; systemic use is nephrotoxic
- Colistin = the only systemically used polypeptide here
