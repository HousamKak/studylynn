### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Tetracyclines

---

**MECHANISM BOX (blue, top):**
- **Class:** **30S** protein-synthesis inhibitor → **bacteriostatic**
- Bind 30S → **block aminoacyl-tRNA from the A (acceptor) site** on the mRNA-ribosome complex
- Entry needs an **energy-dependent uptake** process **absent in mammals** → affinity for microbial ribosome **≫ mammalian** (selective)
- Natural = **oxytetracycline**; semi-synthetic = **doxycycline / tetracycline**

---

## CARD 1 — SPECTRUM (green)
- **Broad-spectrum** + treats **Mycoplasma**
- Common targets: **Rickettsia, Staphylococcus, Pseudomonas, Bacteroides, Clostridia** (anaerobes)

## CARD 2 — DOXYCYCLINE = THE BEST ONE (blue + red highlight)
- **Most lipophilic** → broadest spectrum → **drug of choice**
- Excreted **mainly in bile → intestine** → **renal-safe** (others = renal excretion, minimal metabolism)
- Oral absorption **NOT** reduced by food (unlike the others)
- ⚠️ **IV doxycycline is FATAL in HORSES** — sudden cardiac arrhythmias (red)

## CARD 3 — PHARMACOKINETICS
- Oral absorption **↓ with food** by **chelation** (divalent cations) — except doxycycline
- PO **higher in ruminants** (rumen microflora disrupted)
- Cross **CNS + placenta**; found in **milk**

---

## RESISTANCE — THREE ROUTES (blue, numbered)
1. **↓ accumulation** — efflux or ↓ influx (transport proteins)
2. **Ribosomal protection** — protein prevents binding, or **target mutation**
3. **Enzymatic inactivation** by bacterial enzymes

---

## TOXICITY (red box, organized by system)
- **GI** — most common (cats: **esophageal stricture**; ⚠️ **AVOID in horses → colitis**)
- **Renal necrosis** — high dose / high temp / **outdated** drug (dogs + ruminants); doxycycline = fewer renal effects
- **IV rapid** → **hypotension & collapse** (painful; oxytetracycline OK as IM)
- ⚠️ **IV doxycycline → fatal in horses** (cardiac arrhythmias)
- **Chelates bone & teeth** → halts **long-bone growth** → contraindicated in **pregnancy / young**
- **Superinfection**; **hypersensitivity** (esp. cats)

---

**YELLOW COMPARISON STRIP**

| Feature | Doxycycline | Oxy / chlor / tetracycline |
|---|---|---|
| Lipophilicity | High | Lower |
| Food effect | None | ↓ absorption (chelation) |
| Excretion | **Bile → intestine** | Renal |
| Renal safety | Safer | Riskier (high dose) |
| Horse IV | **FATAL** | avoid (colitis) |

---

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **IV doxycycline = FATAL in horses** (cardiac arrhythmia)
- **Chelation** → ↓ oral absorption with food/cations + binds **bone & teeth** → avoid in young/pregnant
- **GI** is the #1 adverse effect; **avoid all tetracyclines in horses** (colitis)
- **Energy-dependent uptake** = the basis of selective bacterial toxicity

---

**GRAY FOOTER (summary pearls):**
- 30S → block A-site → bacteriostatic, broad spectrum incl. Mycoplasma
- **Doxycycline** = most lipophilic, biliary excretion, renal-safe, food-independent
- Chelation defines both its food interaction and bone/teeth binding
- Resistance = efflux / ribosomal protection / enzymatic inactivation
