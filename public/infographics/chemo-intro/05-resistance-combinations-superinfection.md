### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Resistance, Combinations, Superinfection & Misuse

**RESISTANCE — FOUR MECHANISMS (blue, central bacterial-cell diagram):**
Resistance can arise at any step by which a drug reaches and binds its target —
- **① The drug doesn't reach its target:** reduced entry into the pathogen · enhanced **efflux pumps**
- **② The drug isn't active:** microbial **enzymes destroy** it · altered proteins that should activate a **pro-drug**
- **③ The target is altered:** altered target proteins
- **④ Alternative pathway** bypasses the inhibited step
- ⚠️ Every major antibiotic class now faces significant resistance (red)

## COMBINATION THERAPY (yellow + blue)
- **Use drugs with DIFFERENT mechanisms of action**
- **Synergy/additivity:** bactericidal drugs from different classes tend to combine well
- **Antagonism:** bacteriostatic + bactericidal — mainly relevant when the bactericidal agent inhibits **cell-wall synthesis** (needs growing cells)
- **Indications (when 2+ are justified):** empirical therapy of unknown cause · polymicrobial infection · synergy · "prevent" resistance to monotherapy
- **Disadvantages (red):** ↑ toxicity · selection of multidrug-resistant organisms · loss of normal flora → **superinfection** · ↑ cost

**SUPERINFECTION (red box):**
- A **new infection appearing during treatment** of a primary one
- Cause: removal of normal flora (which makes antibacterial substances + competes for nutrients)
- New organisms are often **drug-resistant bacteria or fungi**
- ↑ Risk with **broader spectrum** and **longer** treatment → choose the **narrowest** effective antibiotic

**MISUSES OF ANTIBIOTICS (gray/red list):**
- Treating non-responsive (e.g. viral) infections · therapy of **fever of unknown origin** · improper dosage · relying on chemotherapy alone · lack of bacteriological information

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- 4 resistance routes: ↓entry · efflux · enzyme/inactivation · altered target (+ bypass pathway)
- Don't combine bacteriostatic + cell-wall-active bactericidal (antagonism)
- Broad-spectrum + long courses → superinfection → use the narrowest drug

**GRAY FOOTER (summary):** resistance hits every class; combine only with rationale (different mechanisms); narrow + short to avoid superinfection; avoid the classic misuses.
