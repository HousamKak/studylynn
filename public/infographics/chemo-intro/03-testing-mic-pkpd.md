### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Will It Work? In-Vitro MIC, In-Vivo PK/PD & Dosing

**IN-VITRO TESTING — "easy" (blue):**
- Lab: **identify the microorganism** + **antimicrobial susceptibility testing**
- **Disk diffusion** → only qualitative/semi-quantitative: **Resistant / Intermediate / Susceptible**
- **Dilution tests** (serial concentrations, agar/broth) → quantitative
- **MIC = Minimum Inhibitory Concentration** = lowest concentration preventing visible growth after 18–24 h (green highlight)

**IN-VIVO ACTIVITY — "complicated" (blue, requirements):**
- Microorganism must be **susceptible** to the drug's mechanism
- Effective concentration must **reach the infection site AND the target in the germ**
- …and **stay long enough** for the cidal/static effect
- …while staying **below the level toxic to eukaryotic (host) cells**
- Achievable **serum concentration** guides S/R calls — but site concentration may be much lower

**⚠️ LOCAL FACTORS THAT REDUCE ACTIVITY (red):**
- **Pus, abscesses, foreign body** at the site → low pH, high protein, anaerobic conditions impair the drug

**DOSING — TIME vs CONCENTRATION DEPENDENT (yellow comparison + curve):**
| | Time-dependent | Concentration-dependent |
|---|---|---|
| What matters | **Time above MIC** (T>MIC) | **Peak height** (high Cmax) |
| Best dosing | Fractionated / frequent (curve B) | Large single dose (curve A) |

*(Same total AUC0–24h; fractionating increases total time above MIC.)*

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **MIC** = lowest conc. preventing visible growth (18–24 h)
- Pus / abscess / foreign body sabotage in-vivo activity
- Time-dependent → maximize **T>MIC** (frequent); concentration-dependent → maximize **peak** (big dose)

**GRAY FOOTER (summary):** in vitro = MIC (easy); in vivo = reach + stay + non-toxic + beware local factors; dose by time- vs concentration-dependence.
