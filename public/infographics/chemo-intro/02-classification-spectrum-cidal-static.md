### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Classifying Antibiotics · Mechanism, Spectrum & Cidal vs Static

**HOW WE CLASSIFY ANTIBIOTICS (green, 4 ways):**
- **Chemical structure** — β-lactams, sulfonamides, fluoroquinolones…
- **Spectrum of activity** — Gram −ve, Gram +ve, anaerobes, broad spectrum…
- **Targeted microorganism** — anti-Staph, anti-Clostridium, anti-Pseudomonas…
- **Time of appearance (generation)** — cephalosporins 1st, 2nd, 3rd… gen
- *Within a family, molecules are similar but NOT identical: spectra, PK, adverse effects differ*

**MECHANISM → SPECTRUM (blue, central bacterial-cell diagram):**
- **The spectrum of activity depends on the mechanism of action**
- Target sites on/in the bacterial cell: **cell wall · cell membrane · ribosome (protein synthesis) · DNA/nucleic acids · folate pathway**

**BACTERIOSTATIC vs BACTERICIDAL (yellow comparison — central):**
| | Bacteriostatic | Bactericidal |
|---|---|---|
| Action | Inhibits growth/replication | Kills the organism |
| When sufficient | Host defenses **intact** | Host defenses **impaired** |

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Spectrum is determined by the **mechanism of action**
- **Bacteriostatic** suffices if host defenses are intact; **bactericidal** required if impaired
- A pharmacological family ≠ identical molecules (spectrum/PK/adverse effects vary)

**GRAY FOOTER (summary):** classify by structure/spectrum/target/generation; mechanism dictates spectrum; static (intact host) vs cidal (impaired host).
