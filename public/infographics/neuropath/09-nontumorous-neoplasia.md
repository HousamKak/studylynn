## UPPER HALF — NON-TUMOROUS & COMPRESSIVE LESIONS

### Card 1 — Melanosis
- **Definition:** accumulation of **black pigment** in brain
- **Species:** young sheep + pigs
- **Type:** congenital
- **Course:** regresses over time (benign)

### Card 2 — Cerebellar Hernia
- **Lesion:** cerebellum takes on a **wedge/triangular shape**
- **Mechanism:** compression of the **medulla oblongata** through foramen magnum
- **⚠️ Outcome:** lethal

### Card 3 — Cholesterol Granuloma
- **Species:** horses
- **Lesion:** granulomatous reaction in ventricles around **cholesterol crystals**
- **Cause:** repeated hemorrhages
- **Consequence:** CSF accumulation → **hydrocephalus**

### Card 4 — Ossifying Pachymeningitis
- **Lesion:** **bony plaques in the meninges**
- **Cause:** chronic compression
- **Effect:** rigid meningeal thickening

## MIDDLE STRIP — SPINAL COMPRESSION DISORDERS

Center the section around a labeled spine diagram showing the relevant vertebral levels.

### Card 5 — Wobbler Syndrome (Spinal Stenosis)

- **Species:** large dogs + horses
- **In horses:**
  - **Dynamic form:** linked to **vertebral instability C3–C5** → osteochondrosis + vertebral subluxation, very young (8–18 months)
  - **Static form:** **hypertrophy of ligamentum flavum**, C6–C7
- **In dogs:**
  - Affects **C5–C6 or C6–C7**
  - Osteochondrosis + thickening of ligamentum flavum
  - Young dogs (1y → 4y)
- **Pathology:**
  - Spinal cord blockage → nerve flow blockage → locomotor disturbances → ascending → sensory damage
  - Axonal degeneration at the level of **white matter**
  - **Wallerian degeneration** both upstream and downstream
  - Loss of myelin → **spheroid holes**

### Card 6 — Vertebral Disc Disease

- **Anatomy:** vertebral disc = **nucleus pulposus + annulus fibrosus**, connected to longitudinal ligaments that maintain the vertebral axis

**Two types (side-by-side comparison):**

**Hansen Type I (Chondrodystrophic breeds — e.g. Dachshund, Beagle)**
- Genetic
- Age: **3–7 years**
- **Rapid cartilaginous metaplasia** of disc pulp → loss of elasticity → rupture → **dorsal disc herniation** → **acute spinal cord compression**
- Often accompanied by **fibrocartilaginous emboli**

**Hansen Type II (Non-chondrodystrophic breeds — large breed dogs)**
- Cause: **age-related**
- **Progressive** cartilaginous metaplasia of nucleus pulposus
- Loss of elasticity → **hypertrophy of annulus fibrosus + ligamentum flavum** → chronic spinal cord compression

- **Common lesion:** degeneration + medullary necrosis
- **Common location:** **T12–L2** (thoracolumbar junction)

## LOWER HALF — NEOPLASIA (6 cards)

### Card 7 — Astrocytoma
- **Gross:** necrotic tissue in brain
- **Color:** gray
- **Consistency:** **firm and hemorrhagic**

### Card 8 — Oligodendrocytoma
- **Gross:** similar location to astrocytoma but
- **Color:** **red**
- **Consistency:** **gelatinous and soft**

### Card 9 — Choroid Plexus Tumor
- **Gross:** firm, red, multinodular proliferation in ventricles
- **Shape:** **cauliflower-shaped**
- **Behavior:** malignant

### Card 10 — Meningioma
- **Gross:** well-defined mass that appears "outside" (extra-axial)
- **Consistency:** firm and granular
- **Treatment:** if ↑ intracranial pressure → surgical operation

### Card 11 — Chondroma
- **⚠️ PATHOGNOMONIC (red box):** appears like the **"tail tip of a ferret"**
- **Treatment:** remove tumor

### Card 12 — Brain Metastases (two key examples)

- **Melanoma:** proliferation of tumor melanocytes — very dark color
- **Hemangiosarcoma:** malignant tumor of blood vessels — metastases accompanied by **hemorrhages**

| Comparison | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Congenital pigment / compression / tumor** | Melanosis (pigment) // Cerebellar hernia (compression) // Astrocytoma (tumor) |
| **Extra-axial vs Intra-axial** | Extra-axial = meningioma // Intra-axial = astrocytoma, oligodendrocytoma |
| **Firm vs Soft vs Gelatinous** | Firm = astrocytoma, meningioma // Soft + gelatinous = oligodendrocytoma // Cauliflower-multinodular = choroid plexus tumor |
| **Hansen I vs II** | Type I = chondrodystrophic, acute, 3–7y // Type II = non-chondrodystrophic, age-related, progressive |

- Combined poster: structural defects (melanosis, hernia, granuloma, pachymeningitis) + compressive spine disease (Wobbler, IVDD) + tumors

- Most CNS tumors kill via mass effect → ↑ ICP → herniation
- Hansen I = acute young chondrodystrophic; Hansen II = chronic old large breed
