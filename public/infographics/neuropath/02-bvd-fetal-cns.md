### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** BVDV and Fetal Nervous System Lesions

---

**AGENT IDENTIFICATION BOX (green, top-left):**
- **Pestivirus**, family **Flaviviridae**
- Two genotypes: **BVDV1** and **BVDV2**
- Two biotypes:
  - **NCP** = Non-Cytopathic form
  - **CP** = Cytopathic form (mutated from NCP)
- Affects: cattle (cows / calves / fetuses)

---

**NON-PREGNANT INFECTION BOX (small side panel, blue):**
- **BVDV NCP — subclinical form:** transient viral disease in cattle, stimulates immune system
- **BVDV NCP — adult severe form:** thrombocytopenic syndrome + lesions similar to mucosal disease

---

**HERO ELEMENT — HORIZONTAL FETAL GESTATIONAL TIMELINE**

Display three colored zones on a long horizontal axis representing days of bovine gestation. Use a fetus icon at each stage that grows in size as days advance.

**Zone 1 — Fetus < 100 days**
- Outcomes: abortion / histiocytic response
- Virus tropism for replicating cells
- Result: early embryonic loss

**Zone 2 — Fetus between 100 and 170 days (CRITICAL CNS WINDOW)**
- Outcome: abortion OR survival with malformations
- Mechanism:
  - Tropism for **blood vessels + neuroblasts**
  - **Vasculitis → edema → hydrocephalus**
  - **Neuroblast degeneration → necrosis → porencephaly**
- ⚠️ **PATHOGNOMONIC LESIONS (red box):**
  - Cerebellar hypoplasia
  - Cerebral malformation
  - Ocular malformation
  - Otic lesion

**Zone 3 — Fetus > 170 days**
- Birth of **PI calf** (Persistently Infected / Immunotolerant calf)
- Mechanism:
  - Immune system recognizes NCP virus as **self**
  - Atrophy of thymus and lymph nodes
- If reinfected with CP variant → **NCP ↔ CP coexistence** → development of **Mucosal Disease**
- Time window for mucosal disease: **6 months → 2 years of age**

---

**MECHANISM FLOW (blue arrows, beneath the timeline)**

A 2-track flow diagram showing parallel pathogenesis pathways during the 100–170 day window:

**Track A — Vascular:**
Virus tropism → vessels → vasculitis → edema → ↑ intracranial pressure → **hydrocephalus**

**Track B — Neuronal:**
Virus tropism → neuroblasts → degeneration → necrosis → cavitation → **porencephaly + cerebellar hypoplasia**

---

**SPECIAL HIGH-YIELD BOX — THE PI CALF (red, prominent)**

- Persistently Infected (PI) calf only forms if infection happens **AFTER 170 days**
- Immunotolerant — recognizes NCP virus as self
- Sheds virus throughout life → reservoir for herd
- Late mutation (NCP → CP) OR re-infection with CP → **fatal Mucosal Disease**
- Window for mucosal disease onset: 6 months → 2 years

---

**BOTTOM SUMMARY STRIP (gray footer):**
- Pestivirus crosses placenta → effects depend ENTIRELY on gestational age
- Before 100 days → death/abortion
- 100–170 days → CNS malformations (pathognomonic tetrad)
- After 170 days → PI calf with risk of mucosal disease
