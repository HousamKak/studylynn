### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Congenital Malformations of the CNS

**CORE DEFINITION BOX (green, top-left):**
- Developmental defects occurring during fetal/embryonic life
- Affect brain and spinal cord
- Causes: genetic, viral, toxic, nutritional, teratogenic

---

**MAIN DISORDERS GRID — 8 mini cards, each with: name, what it is, main cause, species, characteristic lesion**

**1. Cyclopia**
- What: single midline orbit, fusion of eye structures
- Cause: poisoning by *Veratrum californicum* at day 14 of gestation
- Species: sheep
- Lesion: midline craniofacial fusion defect

**2. Lissencephaly**
- What: smooth brain, no convolutions
- Cause: neuronal migration problem
- Lesion: absent gyri and sulci

**3. Dysraphism (neural tube defects)**
- What: failure of neural tube closure during organogenesis
- Affects: skin + spinal canal
- Cause: hereditary in pigs / hereditary + griseofulvin administration in 1st week of gestation in cats
- Subtypes:
  - Spina bifida → spine not closed
  - Cranium bifidum → skull/brain not closed
  - Myelocele → herniation of spinal cord
  - Encephalocele → herniation of brain

**4. Cerebellar Hypoplasia**
- Cats: Feline Panleukopenia Virus (FPV)
  - Tropism for replicating cells → neuroblasts of cerebellum → become necrotic → no migration → hypoplasia
- Pigs + cattle: Pestivirus (BVDV / Border disease / CSFV)

**5. Hydromyelia / Syringomyelia**
- Hydromyelia: dilation of the ependymal canal
- Syringomyelia: additional canal parallel to the ependymal canal (no communication with central canal)

**6. Hydrocephalus**
- CNS atrophy by compression: enlarged ventricles → ↑ volume → ↑ pressure on surrounding tissue (including corpus callosum) → white matter atrophy
- Two etiologies:
  - **Congenital:** genetic in brachycephalic breeds OR viral infection during fetal development — virus tropism for ependymal cells → necrosis + inflammation → scarring → stenosis → hydrocephalus → deformation of skull
  - **Acquired:** cattle + pigs → vitamin A deficiency, impaired drainage of CSF from arachnoid mater, or accumulation following brain lesions
- Three types:
  - **Internal** = ventricular system
  - **External** = arachnoid spaces
  - **Communicating** = both

**7. Porencephaly**
- Cysts in white matter, appear as holes connected to ventricles
- Cause: destruction of immature neuroblasts by virus during fetal life
- Agents: Bluetongue virus, BVDV, Akabane, Border disease virus, copper deficiency

**8. Hydranencephaly**
- Necrosis and absence of cerebral hemispheres → replaced by a cavity containing leptomeninges + CSF
- Animal is born alive because brainstem is spared
- Same causes as porencephaly

---

**SPECIAL CARD — Swayback and Enzootic Ataxia (place in main grid as a 9th card or in a side panel)**
- Demyelination + axonal degeneration in CNS
- Cause: copper deficiency
- **Swayback** = congenital form
- **Enzootic ataxia** = appears during first 6 months
- Extra signs: depigmented coat (Cu is coenzyme of melanin synthesis), osteoporosis

---

**BOTTOM COMPARISON STRIP (yellow):**

| Comparison | Key |
|---|---|
| **Porencephaly vs Hydranencephaly** | Porencephaly = focal cysts in white matter, hemispheres present. Hydranencephaly = complete absence of hemispheres, replaced by CSF cavity |
| **Hydromyelia vs Syringomyelia** | Hydromyelia = ependymal canal dilated. Syringomyelia = parallel cavity, no communication with ependymal canal |
| **Internal vs External hydrocephalus** | Internal = ventricles. External = arachnoid spaces. Communicating = both |
