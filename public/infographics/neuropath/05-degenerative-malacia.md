## FOUR LARGE DISEASE CARDS

Each card should contain: **cause / species / lesion distribution / mechanism / hallmark signs**.

---

### CARD 1 — Polioencephalomalacia

- **Species:** ruminants < 18 months
- **Cause:** thiamine **precursor** deficiency (NOT true thiamine deficiency); also from **lead poisoning** or **water poisoning**
- **Lesion timeline:**
  - Day 2: cerebral edema in **posterior cortex**
  - Cerebral hemispheres become **gelatinous and red** (convulsions described as red)
  - Day 4: **laminar cortical necrosis** revealed by **autofluorescence** under UV light
- **Clinical:**
  - Normal eye anatomy but animal is **centrally blind** (cortical blindness)
- **Hallmark:** autofluorescent laminar necrosis under UV → high-yield diagnostic clue

---

### CARD 2 — Thiamine Deficiency

- **Species:** carnivores (cats especially)
- **Causes:**
  - **Thiaminases** — raw fish diet
  - Heat destruction
  - **Sulfur dioxide** preservation
- **Mechanism (blue):**
  - Defect in enzymes involved in **sugar metabolism**
  - Lack of energy
  - **Oxidative stress**
- **Lesions:**
  - **Holes in periventricular gray matter**
  - **Vacuolation of neutrophils**
  - **Myocardial degeneration** by hemorrhagic necrosis

---

### CARD 3 — Leukoencephalomalacia

- **Species:** horses
- **Cause:** **Fumonisin B1** toxin produced by fungi:
  - *Fusarium proliferatum*
  - *Fusarium verticillioides*
- **Mechanism (blue):**
  - Lipid peroxidation
  - ↓ Synthesis of sphingolipids → **myelin damage**
- **Lesion distribution:**
  - **Brain:** white matter → gray matter necrosis
  - **Spinal cord:** gray → white necrosis
  - **Bilateral but NOT symmetric** (important detail)
- **Associated:** liver damage

---

### CARD 4 — Nigropallidal Encephalomalacia

- **Species:** horses
- **Cause:** toxin **Repin** from plant ***Centaurea repens***
- **Mechanism (blue):**
  - **Glutathione depletion** → oxidative stress
- **Lesion distribution:**
  - **Symmetric**, reaches **substantia nigra** and **globus pallidus** (the name says it)
- **Clinical:**
  - Anorexia + dehydration → death
