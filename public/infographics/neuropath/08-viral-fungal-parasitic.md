## COLUMN 1 — VIRAL (purple accent)

### Card 1.1 — Feline Infectious Peritonitis (FIP)
- **Cause:** feline coronavirus (mutated form)
- **Pathology:** **pyogranulomatous formation** in brain and choroid plexus
- Two forms:
  - **Dry form:** pyogranulomatous encephalitis
  - **Wet form:** modified liquid transudate (effusive)

### Card 1.2 — Equine Rhinopneumonitis
- **Agent:** **Equine Herpesvirus 1 / 4 (EHV-1/4)**
- **Transmission:** inhalation
- **Tropism:** endothelial cells, epithelia, neurons, bone marrow
- **Lesions:** vascular lesions → hemorrhages → necrosis

### Card 1.3 — Aujeszky's Disease (Pseudorabies)
- **Agent:** **Porcine Herpesvirus 1**
- **Species:**
  - **Pigs:** complete cycle — abortions + nerve damage (NOT zoonotic)
  - **Carnivores (dogs/cats):** dead-end host — lesions in **spinal cord**, severe **itching/pruritus** → "mad itch"
- **Latency:** tonsils, olfactory bulb, trigeminal nerve
- **Transmission:** inhalation; in carnivores from raw infected meat or fights between dogs
- **Outcome in carnivores:** no treatment → **euthanize**
- **⚠️ PATHOGNOMONIC (red):** intranuclear inclusion bodies in neurons + condensed/marginalized chromatin

### Card 1.4 — Rabies
- **Agent:** **Rhabdovirus** (Lyssavirus)
- **Transmission:** wounds with infected saliva; inhalation of feces of pipistrelle bats
- **Pathology:** lymphocytic and plasmacytic encephalitis + **intracytoplasmic inclusion bodies (Negri bodies)**
- **Two clinical forms:**
  - **Furious form**
  - **Paralytic form**
- **Delay** between infection and reaching CNS (depends on wound location distance from CNS)

### Card 1.5 — Canine Distemper
- **Agent:** **Canine Distemper Virus** (Paramyxovirus, *Morbillivirus*)
- **Transmission:** inhalation
- **Tropism:** epithelial cells, macrophages, lymphoid organs, CNS
- **Three clinical forms** (acute, subacute, chronic)
- **CNS pathology:** demyelination of oligodendrocytes → locomotor signs + tremors in **cerebellar gray matter**
- **Late form:** virus can "wake up" years later (post-vaccine or post-inhalation)
- **Histology:** non-suppurative encephalitis, **syncytial cells**, **intranuclear inclusion bodies**
- **Other injuries:** pneumonia, periodontal disease, hyperkeratosis (hard pad disease)

### Card 1.6 — Visna-Maedi
- **Agent:** **Retrovirus** (Lentivirus)
- **Species:** adult sheep / young sheep via **colostrum**
- **Pathogenesis:** latency in monocytes → viremia
  - **Visna** = CNS involvement
  - **Maedi** = respiratory involvement
- **Lesions:** spinal cord — **white matter or cerebellum** lesions
- **Pathology:** non-suppurative leukoencephalomyelitis with **severe demyelination**

## COLUMN 2 — FUNGAL (green accent)

### Card 2.1 — Cryptococcus neoformans
- **Form:** yeast
- **Source:** **pigeon droppings**
- **Transmission:** inhalational
- **Predisposing factor:** immunodeficiency
- **Tropism:** **catecholamines** in brain and adrenal glands
- **Defense mechanism:** capsule contains **mannitol** or **sialic acid** → resistance to host immunity
- **Pathology:** **pyogranulomatous inflammation + gelatinous cysts** in brain

## COLUMN 3 — PARASITIC (orange accent)

### Card 3.1 — Equine Protozoal Encephalomyelitis (EPM)
- **Agent:** ***Sarcocystis neurona***
- **Transmission:** oral
- **Pathology:** pyogranulomatous inflammation with necrosis and hemorrhages — at the level of the **spinal cord**

### Card 3.2 — Toxoplasma gondii
- **Transmission:**
  - Horizontal (oral)
  - Vertical (transplacental)
- **Hosts:**
  - **Cat = definitive host**
  - All other mammals = intermediate hosts
- **Pathology:** **bradyzoite cysts** in brain → inflammation + necrosis

### Card 3.3 — Neospora caninum
- **Definitive hosts:** dogs and coyotes
- **Transmission:** vertical (reactivation of latent infection during gestation)
- **Clinical:** reproductive + neurological disorders
- **Pathology:**
  - Dogs → pyogranulomatous encephalitis
  - Calves → necrotizing encephalitis

### Card 3.4 — Coenurus cerebralis
- **Agent:** Cestode (tapeworm) — *Taenia multiceps* larval stage
- **Host:** sheep
- **Lesion:** cysts in sheep brain, **frontal and parietal lobes**
- **Clinical name:** "**Dizziness disease**" / Gid in sheep

### Card 3.5 — Babesia
- **Type:** parasite of **erythrocytes**
- **Pathogenesis:** infected RBCs block passage through **smallest capillaries** → cerebral babesiosis with ischemic-type lesions
