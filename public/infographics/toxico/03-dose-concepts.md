**OVERVIEW BOX (green, top):**
- Toxicity is **dose-dependent** — every substance has a threshold
- Doses are expressed in **mg/kg body weight** (or per day)
- Always specify **species** and **route** (oral, IV, SC, IP, inhalation)

---

## DOSE-RESPONSE LADDER (centerpiece — vertical stack, bottom = safe, top = lethal)

### 🟢 ADI — Acceptable Daily Intake (green, base of ladder)
- Amount a **human** can ingest daily, lifelong, with **NO risk**
- Expressed in **mg/kg/day**
- Derived from **NOAEL ÷ safety coefficient (100 or 1000)**

### 🟡 MRL — Maximum Residue Limit (yellow)
- Concentration of residues that must **NOT be exceeded in food** (animal / plant origin)
- Used in **international food trade**
- Expressed in **ppm = mg residue / kg food**
- Established by **WHO / FAO**

### 🟢 NOAEL — No Observed Adverse Effect Level (green)
- Highest repeated dose (30 days–2 years) causing **NO** biochemical / microscopic / macroscopic / clinical abnormality
- Reference for setting **ADI**

### 🟡 MTD — Maximum Tolerated Dose (yellow)
- Highest dose in LD50 studies with **NO MORTALITY**
- Symptoms of poisoning are still **observed**
- ≠ NOAEL — MTD allows symptoms, NOAEL does not

### 🔴 LD50 — Lethal Dose 50% (red, top of ladder)
- Single dose killing **50% of test animals**
- Expressed in **mg/kg**, species- and route-specific
- Sometimes applied to repeated dosing within 24 h
- **Repeated dosing can ↑↑ toxicity** — e.g. coumafen: 50 mg/kg single dose vs **5 mg/kg/day × 5–10 days**

---

## UNIT CONVERSION SIDEBAR (blue box)

**Solid samples**
- **1 ppm** = 1 mg/kg = 1 µg/g
- **1 ppb** = 1 µg/kg = 1 ng/g
- 1 ppm = **1000 ppb**

**Gaseous compounds**
- ppm = **1 mL gas / m³ air** (volume-based)
- Some prefer **vpm** (volume per million) for gases
- **mg/m³** = ppm × gas density

**Sample state matters**
- Concentration relative to **fresh** vs **dried** sample differs
- Example: 80% water content sample → fresh 1 ppm = dried 0.2 ppm

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **LD50 = single dose / 50% kill** — always state **species + route**
- **Coumafen** = classic example of repeated-dose toxicity (50 mg/kg single vs 5 mg/kg/day × 5–10 d)
- ADI = **NOAEL ÷ 100 (or 1000)** — safety margin for humans
- ppm = **mg/kg** for solids, **mL/m³** for gases
- For gases: **mg/m³ = ppm × density**

---

**GRAY FOOTER (summary pearls):**
- LD50 = lethal end. ADI = safe end.
- Repeated dosing **lowers** the effective lethal dose dramatically
- ppm always needs context: solid (mass) vs gas (volume), fresh vs dry
- Animals + humans share the framework; ADI/MRL are human-facing
