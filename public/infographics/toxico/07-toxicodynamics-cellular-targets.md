### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Toxicodynamics — Cellular Targets & DNA Adducts

---

**CORE CONCEPT BOX (green, top):**
- **Toxicodynamics** = what the **toxin does to the body**
- Toxic effect = **toxicoavailable entity** × **endogenous receptor concentration**
- Toxicoavailable entities = parent toxin + reactive metabolites (electrophiles, radicals) that escaped detox
- They bind covalently to **nucleophilic** sites in cells

## MOLECULAR TARGETS (right column)

Toxins react with **nucleophilic sites** on endogenous biomolecules:

**Proteins (enzymes, structural, transport, immune)**
- Enzyme inhibition / stimulation / diversion
- **Collagen** (structural)
- **Hemoglobin** (oxygen transport — blocked)
- Glycoproteins → immune dysfunction, hypersensitization

**Nucleic acids (DNA + RNA)**
- **DNA adducts** → tumor / teratogenic
- **Guanine** = most-reactive base (highest frequency of toxicodynamic reactions)
- T, A, C, U also have nucleophilic centers, but **less reactive**

**Lipids + Phospholipids**
- Membrane integrity loss
- Plasmalogen denaturation (methylmercury)

**Coenzymes**
- **Coenzyme A**
- **Lipoic acid** (arsenic target)

**Vitamins (consumed/blocked)**
- Thiamine (B1)
- Pyridoxine (B6) — crimidine antagonist
- Ascorbic acid (C)
- Tocopherol (E)

**Glutathione**
- The cell's main detoxification reservoir — depleted by reactive metabolites

## DNA ADDUCT FOCUS — BENZO[a]PYRENE (red panel, lower-left)

**Source:** coal tar, biomass combustion, vehicle exhaust, **tobacco smoke**, grilled / barbecued meat, toast

**Properties:**
- **PAH** (polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon)
- Yellow crystalline solid, **5 fused rings**
- Air pollutant, persistent
- **Highly mutagenic and carcinogenic**

**Mechanism (blue):**
1. Inhaled / ingested
2. Lung cells convert it → **Benzo(a)pyrene-diol-epoxide (BPDE)**
3. BPDE **intercalates** into DNA
4. Modifies **guanine** → deaminates it
5. Mutant base now pairs with **adenine** (not cytosine)
6. **G:C → A:T transversion** → permanent mutation
7. Lung malignancy

⚠️ Tracer molecule for all PAHs in environmental sampling

---

## DNA ADDUCT FOCUS — AFLATOXIN B1 (red panel, lower-right)
- **Aspergillus flavus** mycotoxin (moldy peanuts, grain)
- Bioactivated by liver CYP450 → **AFB1-8,9-epoxide**
- Binds **N7 of guanine** → DNA adduct
- → hepatocellular carcinoma
- One of the most potent **natural carcinogens** known

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- Toxic damage spans **simple allergy → cell necrosis → irreversible mutation**
- **Guanine** is the favorite DNA target (highest nucleophilicity)
- **BaP → BPDE → G:A mispair** = textbook smoking-lung-cancer mechanism
- **Aflatoxin B1 → N7-guanine adduct** = hepatocellular carcinoma
- Reactive metabolite, not the parent, often does the damage

---

**GRAY FOOTER (summary pearls):**
- Toxicoavailable entity (parent + escaped reactive metabolite) + nucleophilic target = damage
- Subcellular targets: **membrane, ER, mito, nucleus**
- Molecular targets: **proteins, nucleic acids, lipids, coenzymes, vitamins, glutathione**
- Mutagenicity = **DNA adduct → mispair → mutation**
- BaP + aflatoxin B1 = the two flagship adduct examples
