## CENTRAL VISUAL — 5 EXIT ARROWS

A simplified animal body in the center. Five arrows radiate outward to 5 cards:

---

### 🟢 ROUTE 1 — RENAL (urine)
- Removes **polar, water-soluble, dissociated** molecules
- Polarity & pH of urine modulate **tubular reabsorption**
- Some less-polar derivatives still appear: **HCH, DDT** + metabolites
  - HCH = **80% in urine** (rats)
- **Side effects of renal clearance:**
  - **Nephritis** from heavy metals
  - **Oxalate crystals** → renal precipitation (ethylene glycol)

---

### 🟢 ROUTE 2 — DIGESTIVE (with enterohepatic recirculation)

**Outlets:**
- **Bile** → conjugated derivatives, high-MW (> 400 Da)
- **Saliva** → alkaloids, amphetamines, mercury
- **Gastric juice** → basic substances (nicotine)
- Unabsorbed toxin directly in feces

**🔴 ENTEROHEPATIC RECIRCULATION (red callout):**
- Bile-excreted **conjugates** can be hydrolyzed by bile enzymes + intestinal flora
- Resulting **non-polar** products → reabsorbed by intestine → back to liver
- → re-conjugated → secreted again
- Result: **prolonged toxicity, loops indefinitely**
- ⚠️ Up to **90%** of methylmercury excreted in bile can be **reabsorbed**
- **Treatment lever:** block recirculation → enhance fecal excretion (activated charcoal, cholestyramine)

---

### 🟢 ROUTE 3 — PULMONARY (exhaled air)
- Removes **gaseous / volatile** toxins
- Examples:
  - **Volatile hydrocarbons** (halogenated + non-halogenated)
  - **Cyanides**
  - **Carbon oxides** (CO)

---

### 🟢 ROUTE 4 — MAMMARY (milk)
- Removes **fat-soluble** substances
- Examples:
  - **Organophosphate insecticides + carbamates**
  - **Pyrethroids**
  - **Heavy metals**
- ⚠️ Public health: contaminates milk supply → human exposure

---

### 🟢 ROUTE 5 — EGGS (poultry)
- Studied mainly for **organochlorine insecticides**
- Example: hens on **100 ppm DDT** diet →
  - **11–17 ppm** in egg white (albumen)
  - **230–460 ppm** in yolk
- Yolk = **lipid-rich** → concentrates lipophilic toxins

---

**YELLOW COMPARISON STRIP — Routes by Toxin Type**

| Toxin type | Preferred exit |
|---|---|
| Polar, water-soluble | Renal (urine) |
| Conjugated, MW > 400 Da | Biliary → enterohepatic risk |
| Volatile / gaseous | Pulmonary |
| Lipid-soluble (OP, pyrethroid, metals) | Mammary milk |
| Lipophilic (organochlorines) | Egg yolk (poultry) |
| Alkaloids, mercury | Saliva, gastric juice |

---

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **Enterohepatic recirculation** = key reason some toxins linger (Hg, methylmercury)
- **DDT/HCH in eggs** = food-safety + animal-tox concern
- **Mammary excretion** = exposes neonates + milk consumers
- Blocking bile-loop reabsorption is a **therapeutic target**
- Renal excretion can **damage the kidney itself** (heavy metals, oxalates)

---

**GRAY FOOTER (summary pearls):**
- 5 routes: **kidney, digestive, lung, mammary, egg**
- Polar → urine. Conjugates → bile (watch enterohepatic loop). Volatile → lungs.
- Lipophilic toxins follow **milk and yolk** — public-health risk
- Blocking enterohepatic recirculation is a real **therapeutic lever**
