### THIS POSTER — Specific Content

**TITLE (top band):** Distribution & Biotransformation

---

**CORE CONCEPT BOX (green, top):**
- After absorption, toxins **distribute** based on polarity and binding
- **Liver biotransformation** can go two ways:
  - **🟢 Detoxification** → more polar, water-soluble → easier excretion
  - **🔴 Bioactivation** → MORE toxic metabolites

---

## DISTRIBUTION SECTION (left third)

**Where toxins end up depends on dose pattern**

**Single ingestion (acute):**
- The **most vascularized organs** retain the most toxin
- Example: sheep, fatal **HCH** ingestion → brain + liver hold **20 ppm**, fat reserves < 10 ppm

**Repeated low doses:**
- **Adipose tissue affinity dominates**
- Example: sheep on daily lindane → fat **> 100 ppm**, liver/brain < 10 ppm
- ⚠️ Rapid **weight loss** releases stored toxin → **acute poisoning episode**

---

## BIOTRANSFORMATION — TWO PATHWAYS (central spread)

### 🔴 BIOACTIVATION (red panel) — "More toxic than the parent"
Examples (each as a small arrow card):

- **Parathion → Paraoxon** — potent cholinesterase inhibitor
- **Malathion → Malaoxon** — toxicity **× 40** vs parent
- **Methanol → Formic acid** → toxic to **optic nerve** (blindness)
- **Ethylene glycol → Oxalic acid** → kidney damage / crystals
- **Bromobenzene → hydroxylated metabolites** → liver necrosis
- **Aromatic nitro derivatives** + **hydroxylated amines** → **hemolytic** metabolites

⚠️ Initial "harmless" parent = **prototoxin**, danger appears post-metabolism

---

### 🟢 DETOXIFICATION (green panel) — "More polar, more excretable"
Common reactions:
- Hydroxylation + conjugation of halogenated aromatic hydrocarbons
- Salicylates, **barbiturates**
- **Acetylation** of sulfonamides
- Glucuronidation (key safety pathway)

**Result:** molecule becomes more polar / water-soluble → easier renal or biliary excretion

---

## SPECIES & AGE — FACTORS THAT BREAK DETOX (red panel, right side)

**🐱 Cats — deficient in phenol glucuronyl transferase**
- ↓ glucuronidation of hydroxylated aromatic derivatives
- **Phenols accumulate** → liver necrosis
- ⏱ Cat takes **~3 days** to clear phenol vs **~8 h** in dog
- Sources: insecticides, essential oils, human meds, even "cat-safe" products
- Symptoms: salivation, vomiting, seizures, liver necrosis

**Fetuses, neonates, elderly**
- Immature OR declining hepatic enzymes
- ↓ detoxification capacity
- Phenolics + barbiturates accumulate

**Liver microsome stimulation (induction)**
- Repeated substrate exposure → **↑** detox activity
- Cross-reactivity: one drug can speed up clearance of another

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **Biotransformation ≠ always detoxification** — many parents are *prototoxins*
- **Cats + phenols = liver necrosis** (no glucuronyl transferase)
- Lindane in fat → **weight loss = poisoning event**
- Methanol blindness, ethylene glycol kidney damage = **both metabolites, not the parent**
- Malaoxon = **40× parathion** equivalent

- 2 fates in the liver: bioactivation (red) or detoxification (green)
- Species deficiency (cat) + age extremes break detox
- Stored lipophilic toxin = future acute episode waiting on weight loss
