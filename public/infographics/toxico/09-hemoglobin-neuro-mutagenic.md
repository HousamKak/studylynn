## QUADRANT 1 — HEMOGLOBIN ACTION (top-left, red frame)

### Card 1a — Lead → Heme Biosynthesis
- Lead concentrates in **bone marrow** (50× blood level)
- **Inhibits 3 enzymes** of heme synthesis:
  - **ALA dehydratase** → ↑ urinary aminolevulinic acid
  - **Coproporphyrinogen decarboxylase** → ↑ urinary coproporphyrin → **basophilic stippling of RBCs**
  - **Heme synthetase (ferrochelatase)** → protoporphyrin binds Zn → **protoporphyrin-zinc (PPZ) complex** (rises slowly, peaks ~2 months, persists after exposure ends)
- ⟹ ↓ RBC lifespan → **anemia**

### Card 1b — Carbon Monoxide
- **220–250× affinity** vs O2 for hemoglobin
- Forms **HbCO** (carboxyhemoglobin) → cannot transport O2
- Reaction **poorly reversible**

### Card 1c — Methemoglobin-Inducers
- **Chlorates (herbicides), nitrites**
- Oxidize **Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺** in hemoglobin → **methemoglobin**
- Blood turns **chocolate-brown**
- Disrupt redox systems maintaining Fe²⁺
- **Nitrites** also vasodilate → compounds the anoxia
- ⟹ **Tissue anoxia → cyanosis → CNS damage**

## QUADRANT 2 — NEUROTOXIC MECHANISMS (top-right, blue frame)

### Card 2a — Lead (neurotoxic)
Multiple mechanisms:
- Competes with **Ca²⁺** at ganglionic synapses + neuromuscular junctions (ACh + CNS mediator release)
- Interferes with **dopamine reuptake**
- **GABA interference**
- **Demyelination** + peripheral nerve conduction disorders

### Card 2b — Methylmercury (CH₃Hg⁺)
- Hydrolyzes **plasmalogens** (membrane phospholipids specific to neurons)
- Weakens neuronal membranes → **lysis**

### Card 2c — Pyrethroids
- **Non-cyanated** (e.g. permethrin) → block **Na⁺ channels** open → nerve impulse propagates uncontrollably
- **Cyanated** (cyfluthrin, cypermethrin, deltamethrin, fenvalerate) → bind **GABA receptor** → open Cl⁻ channel → **hyperpolarization → inactivation** of the nerve membrane

### Card 2d — Strychnine
- Classic **convulsant**
- Blocks **Renshaw cell** inhibition of motor neurons
- Competitive **antagonist of glycine** (inhibitory neurotransmitter)
- ⟹ Extensor contraction → **opisthotonos** posture

### Card 2e — Crimidine
- Convulsant
- Antagonizes **vitamin B6 (pyridoxine)** in CNS

### Card 2f — Metaldehyde
- ↓ **5-HT (serotonin)** in brain
- ↓ **GABA** in CNS
- Tremors, seizures

## QUADRANT 3 — MUTAGENIC + OXIDATIVE DAMAGE (bottom-left, red frame)

### Card 3a — Paraquat → Superoxide
- Paraquat (and **diquat**) compete with NADP⁺ for electrons
- Reduced paraquat + O₂ → **superoxide ions (O₂•⁻)**
- Superoxide binds **unsaturated lipids** → lipid peroxides
- Peroxides alter **pulmonary surfactant** → respiratory distress
- ⟹ **Irreversible pulmonary fibrosis**

### Card 3b — Methylmercury (mutagenic)
- CH₃Hg⁺ = **electrophilic alkylating agent**
- Alkylates nitrogenous bases (especially **thymine**)
- → DNA denaturation, chromosomal breaks
- **Genetic + teratogenic effects**

### Card 3c — Lindane
- Organochlorine insecticide
- **Chromosomal aberrations** in hamster fibroblast cultures (in vitro)
- Mutagenic / carcinogenic concern

---

## QUADRANT 4 — IMMUNOTOX + ENDOCRINE DISRUPTION (bottom-right, blue frame)

### Card 4a — Immunotoxicity (POPs)
- **Persistent Organic Pollutants** — resist photolytic / biological / chemical degradation
- Cause:
  - **Immunosuppression**
  - **Allergy**
  - **Autoimmune** reactions

### Card 4b — Endocrine Disruptors
- **Exogenous** agents interfering with hormone:
  - Synthesis
  - Secretion
  - Transport
  - Binding
  - Action
  - Elimination
- Disrupt **homeostasis, reproduction, development, behavior**
- Major culprits: **PCBs, dioxins, pesticides**

**RED HIGH-YIELD BOX:**
- **Chocolate-brown blood** = methemoglobinemia (nitrites, chlorates)
- **HbCO** = 220–250× O2 affinity → carbon-monoxide poisoning hallmark
- **Lead PPZ complex** = lab marker, **basophilic stippling** = blood smear marker
- **Opisthotonos** = strychnine (glycine antagonism, Renshaw block)
- **Paraquat lung fibrosis** = irreversible; pulmonary failure is the killer, not the GI burn
- **Methylmercury** = mutagen + neurotoxin + teratogen — triple threat
- **POPs + endocrine disruptors** persist in environment + bioaccumulate

- Four specific-action quadrants: **hemoglobin · neuro · mutagenic · endocrine**

- POPs + endocrine disruptors are the **chronic / population-level** end of veterinary toxicology
- Mechanism-aware diagnosis enables mechanism-targeted antidotes
